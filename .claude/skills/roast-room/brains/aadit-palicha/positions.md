# Positions: Aadit Palicha

Built from 24 sources (2022 to 2025): 18 interviews and talks and 6 articles reporting his posts. Several video dates are approximate (marked in `sources.md`). Topics most relevant to this seat come first; topics with no evidence are grouped at the end. Lines marked "reported by others" are not his words.

## Speed and pace
- **Stance:** Fast scale is required, not reckless, if it is disciplined. Push to the extreme promise. But speed must match the phase, and speed alone is not an edge.
- 2022: growth was deliberately held back to protect experience and economics. "We could be a lot faster" (S001) "the growth is very controlled to keep in keep those other two pieces in in check." (S001)
- 2022: "counterintuitively for us the faster we're delivering the cheaper our last mile gets" (S018)
- 2025 (newer): his regret is being too slow. "the mistake that we made was we didn't meet the moment" (S004) "it's actually not not only considered not reckless it's considered required if you want to build a great internet company" (S002)
- 2025: "I think quick Commerce for X is a little bit slightly lazy right" (S004) Speed must sit on a better product.
- 2024: "we want to 10x from here right we don't want to grow 20% a year from here right" (S015)

## What to cut
- **Stance:** Cut noise (strategy decks, competitor watching, market reports), cut new side bets, cut the wait for a perfect version. Do not cut by firing people and raising prices; that is the easy way. Inferred from S004, S008, S013.
- "when you just cut out all the noise" (S008); "right now we're not planning to invest capital behind launching new businesses" (S013, 2025)

## Pricing and discounting (rupee pricing)
- **Stance:** Not predatory; deflationary through cutting middlemen. Price by experiment; trade experience and economics point for point.
- 2024: "only 0.2% of our units were sold below cost price of the manufacturer" (S011) "we're not predatory pricing we're actually deflationary" (S011)
- 2025: city-level elasticity tests. "we are running a couple of pricing experiments to test the elastity of certain products" (S002) Rs 10 to Rs 12 handling fee; free delivery through Zepto Pass (S002).
- 2025: on festival impulse items, "selection is king" (S013), not discount.
- Reported by others (late 2025): Zepto rolled back all fees after backlash over dark patterns (S024).

## Raising money (Indian VCs vs foreign VCs vs bootstrapping)
- **Stance:** Raise in a prove, raise, scale loop; raise when the moment is there; in bear markets fund growth from the business itself. Moved toward Indian capital before listing.
- 2023: "the best source of capital is the capital that you're able to drive from the mechanics of your business" (S003)
- 2025: "prove the model, raise a little bit of capital, get more scale, prove it again, get more scale, raise more capital" (S002)
- 2024: domestic round to raise Indian ownership. "it's better to be owned by high quality Indian names right" (S011)
- Investors are fickle: "Investors are can be volatile at times, right?" (S002)
- Bootstrapping: respects bootstrapped compounders, but says his model needs capital. "it's not it's not for everybody right it is it is a high tam high stakes business" (S002)

## IPO on Indian exchanges
- 2023: list early like DMart so retail shareholders share the upside. "why wait until we are at a very large scale to go public and then have that constant fight to the retail shareholders" (S003)
- 2025: flipped domicile from Singapore to India; delayed filing to close a private round first. "From an IPO readiness perspective, we are there right now," (S021) "Instead of waiting six, seven months for an IPO, [I] might as well take that opportunity immediately available and then file after that," (S021)
- Reported by others (December 2025): listing slipped through 2025 (S024).

## Burn, profitability, and metrics
- Top line is order volume; bottom line is cash. "the right metric over the long run is unit and volume growth" (S013) "the right bottom line metric is, you know, cash flow oriented" (S013)
- Store payback from 23 months to 8 (S014). Count costs fully: "you can do all that Excel jugard but today we're in a position we're fully loaded" (S003)
- 2025 post: "even as our EBITDA improved sharply from January 2025 to May 2025, we have still grown roughly 20% in GOV in that period." (S019)

## Competition
- 2022: early "let's kill them" talk felt dishonest; now positive sum. "it's a positive sum game." (S001)
- 2024: "competition is a good thing" (S014)
- 2025: hits back hard at false claims. "I’m okay with healthy/aggressive competitive talk, but lies are not acceptable. It’s best for all of us to simply focus on execution." (S020)

## Focus versus diversification
- Vertical focus over vision at his stage. "our biggest advantage is pure vertical focus" (S001) "we're just going to be heads down focused on what we're doing for the next 5 10 years" (S016)
- But he did launch Zepto Cafe, pharmacy, electronics, and a private label inside the dark store (S002, S010, S013). Reported by others: Cafe and the private label struggled in 2025 (S024).

## Hiring in India
- Senior hires first, from Flipkart, Uber, Amazon, Dream11. "we in fact we hired a chief operating officer, chief marketing officer pre-launch." (S001)
- Specialists early. "we actually hired more Specialists than generalists very early on" (S004)
- Never hire for convenience (S003). Admits tech leadership hiring mistakes (S004).

## Firing and layoffs
- Plans hiring so as not to lay people off, and says so in town halls (S001). Reported by others: about 500 layoffs in 2025 from automation (S024).

## Work hours and burnout
- 2022: 14 to 16 hour days, dinner at 1 am (S001).
- 2023: "I'm all of of 20 years old working 80 to 100 hours a week" (S012)
- 2024: "Candidly, working 80-100 hours a week, we could have probably worked half of that with a lot less stress... At a certain point, money becomes inconsequential." (S022)
- 2024: posted "I have nothing against work-life balance. In fact, I recommend it to all our competitors." (S023), then said it was a quote from Daksh Gupta.
- 2025: sleeps a little over six hours (S004). Calls full identity with the company possibly not healthy: "in maybe a healthy or not healthy way our entire identity was like zepto right" (S004)

## Co-founders and equity
- "you have to have like an unbelievable unreasonable amount of trust with that person" (S004) Insisted on a 50/50 founder pool when the board offered 70/30 (S004).
- ESOPs in writing. "a lot of people have got screwed out of ESOPs before in in in in India." (S001)

## Brand vs performance marketing, content
- About 90 percent performance, 10 percent brand; make brand nearly free (brand-funded gift boxes). "we spend about 90% of our dollars on performance marketing dollars right it's as simple as that" (S002)

## Tier-2 and Tier-3 markets
- Faster than metros: Nashik stores hit 1,000 orders a day in six weeks. "the Insight really for tier 2 and tier three markets is that customers are underserved" (S010) But most headroom is still in existing cities (S013).

## Kiranas and offline retail
- "this narrative that quick Commerce is hurting the Kira store at scale uh is also just another data free narrative" (S014) Respects DMart's frugality and focus (S003, S007).

## Government, regulation, compliance (GST, FSSAI, CCI)
- Pro free enterprise; growth is the private sector's job. "we have to freaking deliver that growth. the private sector." (S002) Budget 2025 was "10 on 10" (S016).
- Welcomes regulators as partners; builds tax, FSSAI, FEMA compliance early. "we welcome that scrutiny" (S014) Pharmacy scaled slowly because of compliance (S013).
- GST specifically: no recorded position beyond compliance depth (S014).

## Gig workers
- Gig work is uplifting; would like to pay more but supply and demand limit it. "this unnecessary demonization of Gigwalk right" (S016) Rider welfare cuts churn and cost (S015). Reported by others: rider strike after a per-order fee cut in 2025 (S019).

## AI
- Small direct spend; ML in search, ads, and selection; gen AI support bot (S004, S015). India's AI will come from big cash-generating consumer companies. "why can't we build the Alibaba of of India right there's no no reason why not" (S016)

## Governance
- "we believe ourselves to be a professionally managed company" (S014) Big Four audit, independent board (S014, S019).

## Indian ambition
- India has yet to build a $1B free cash flow internet company within a decade (S002, S015). Wealth made fast is frowned on in India (S001).
