# Principles: Aadit Palicha

Built from 24 sources: 18 long interviews, talks, and TV conversations (2022 to 2025) and 6 articles reporting his posts (2024 to 2025). Quick depth.

22 decision rules, most-used first, with the speed and execution rules lifted to the top for this seat. "Sources" counts distinct sources that state or apply the rule. Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Execution decides; never blame the market.** "all the only thing that matters is freaking execution, right?" (S002) "The second you start seeing CEOs get into that victim mindset, we short the stock, right?" (S002) Sources: 8 (S001, S002, S004, S013, S015, S016, S019, S020). Confidence: high.

2. **Companies die of suicide, not homicide. Look inward, not at rivals.** "you're significantly more likely as a founder to die of suicide than homicide." (S001) "you're 99% more likely to die by Suicide and homicide" (S004) Sources: 2 (S001, S004). Confidence: medium.

3. **Watch your own numbers, not the rival's press release.** "the numbers that are in front of you about your own business are like 99 times more important to determining how big this will be than that press release." (S001) Sources: 3 (S001, S014, S016). Confidence: high.

4. **Be tight before PMF and aggressive right after it.** "pre pmf you want to be highly conservative right and you want to be constrained" (S004) "post pmf just after you find PF you want to be aggressive right" (S004) Sources: 2 (S004, S017). Confidence: medium.

5. **When the moment comes, meet it; staying cautious too long is a mistake.** "the mistake that we made was we didn't meet the moment" (S004) Sources: 2 (S003, S004). Confidence: medium.

6. **Retention first; it is the P&L.** "most consequential pnl metric is retention" (S004) Sources: 7 (S002, S003, S004, S008, S009, S015, S018). Confidence: high.

7. **Go to the doorstep; talk to customers yourself.** "talk to customers" (S002) "how do we get the 30 people around us to order from us?" (S008) Sources: 7 (S002, S004, S008, S009, S012, S014, S017). Confidence: high.

8. **Cut the noise: grand strategy, market maps, and investor opinion can wait.** "when you just cut out all the noise" (S008) "it's all just like intellectual nonsense" (S004) Sources: 3 (S004, S008, S002). Confidence: high.

9. **Take the extreme version of the promise, then make it work.** "we didn't think there was any more anything more extreme than 10 minutes" (S015) Sources: 3 (S008, S015, S018). Confidence: high.

10. **Ship the crude version fast; no launch moment.** "prototype probably would have taken like 72 hours to build" (S009) "There was no like launch moment" (S009) Sources: 3 (S004, S005, S009). Confidence: high.

11. **Growth and profitability are not at odds.** "growth and profitability and not at odds with each other" (S003) "customer love and good execution will always be profitable in my mind" (S004) Sources: 4 (S003, S004, S015, S019). Confidence: high.

12. **Fix economics with better calls for customers, not by firing people and raising prices.** "instead of just like firing people raising prices which is the easy thing to do" (S004) Sources: 1 (S004). Confidence: low.

13. **Burn only where a rupee comes back as two.** "selling a dollar for 90 or are you actually investing a dollar to get $2 back in 6 to 9 months" (S004) Sources: 3 (S003, S004, S014). Confidence: high.

14. **Focus on the core; no new businesses while the core needs capital.** "clarity is our biggest advantage." (S001) "right now we're not planning to invest capital behind launching new businesses" (S013) Sources: 4 (S001, S013, S016, S003). Confidence: high.

15. **Never hire because it is easy. Hire people who get it: in the game, detail-oriented, aggressive.** "we never put ourselves in a position where we hired someone because it is easy and convenient" (S003) "entrepreneurship detail orientation and aggression these three things" (S004) Sources: 3 (S001, S003, S004). Confidence: high.

16. **Do not over-hire to cover execution risk when flush with cash.** "One one classic example of like blitz scaling companies what they do wrong is they over hire." (S001) Sources: 1 (S001). Confidence: low.

17. **Learn other founders' mistakes before you scale.** "spending a lot of time with other founders to just get a very you know, up front what did you do wrong and what can I just not do wrong, right?" (S001) Sources: 2 (S001, S003). Confidence: medium.

18. **Tell the team the bad news first; then they believe the good news.** "the mentality I've had is just or I should try to adopt is radical transparency." (S001) Sources: 2 (S001, S002). Confidence: medium.

19. **Argue with data, not anecdote.** "we're on the side of data and facts" (S011) Sources: 4 (S011, S014, S019, S020). Confidence: high.

20. **Compete hard, but no lies.** "I’m okay with healthy/aggressive competitive talk, but lies are not acceptable. It’s best for all of us to simply focus on execution." (S020) Sources: 3 (S014, S019, S020). Confidence: high.

21. **Merit over age in every debate.** "we are obsessed with meritocracy" (S003) Sources: 2 (S003, S015). Confidence: medium.

22. **Take the conversation that feels beneath you; the associate is faster than the senior partner.** "if I start feeling that things are beneath me, Yeah. that this conversation is beneath me." (S001) Sources: 1 (S001). Confidence: low.
