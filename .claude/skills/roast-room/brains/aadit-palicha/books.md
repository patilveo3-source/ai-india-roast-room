# Books: Aadit Palicha

Built from 24 sources (2022 to 2025). No book text was fetched; summaries are in my own words.

He has written no books. His thinking lives in long interviews and a few LinkedIn and X posts. Below are his recurring talk themes, then the books he says shaped him.

## Recurring themes in his talks

### The Zepto origin and iteration story
- **What it is:** A WhatsApp group for a neighbour in the 2020 lockdown, Kirana Cart, nine months of founders delivering by bicycle, the first dark store in a co-founder's room, then 10-minute delivery.
- **Core idea for this seat:** ship crude, stay at the doorstep, change the model when incremental fixes stop working.
- **Sources:** S003, S004, S008, S009, S012, S015, S017.

### Execution over everything
- **What it is:** His repeated claim that 90 to 99 percent of a company's destiny is its own execution, and that market, macro, and regulators are excuses.
- **Sources:** S001, S002, S004, S013, S016, S020.

### India's missing billion-dollar free cash flow internet company
- **What it is:** India has yet to build an internet company that generates about a billion dollars of free cash flow within a decade, as the US, China, Brazil, and Korea did. Fast, disciplined scale is how to fix that.
- **Sources:** S002, S015, S016.

### Quick commerce defended with data
- **What it is:** His public rebuttals on kiranas, predatory pricing, gig work, and rival claims about burn, each with numbers.
- **Sources:** S011, S014, S016, S019, S020, S021.

## Books he names

- **Thinking in Systems** (Donella Meadows): he recommends it for building the "scenario tree" behind a customer insight (S002).
- **The Toyota Way**: read in 2022 to learn operating excellence and supply chain (S004).
- **Venture Deals** (Brad Feld): speed-read in a couple of days before his first fundraise (S012).
- **Some Sizes Fit All** (Akhil Gupta, Bharti Airtel): named among the best operator books from India (S002).
- **The Hard Thing About Hard Things** and **The Art of War**: named in passing as part of heavy reading through audiobooks (S002).
- **The Techno-Optimist Manifesto** (Marc Andreessen, an essay): the piece he resonated with most; he calls himself an optimistic builder (S002).
- Chartered accountancy textbooks: read to learn finance (S004).
