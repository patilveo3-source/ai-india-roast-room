# Principles: Raghuram Rajan

Built from 28 sources: 13 RBI speeches (2014 to 2016) and 15 interviews, book talks, and keynotes (2016 to 2026). Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Build the environment, then let entrepreneurs choose; never pick sectors.** "Learning from our past, I would say let us not encourage anything; that might be the surest way of killing it." (S025) "Better to focus on creating an enabling environment, and let the core competencies emerge from the fillip given by the environment" (S021) Sources: 9. High.
2. **Do not ask for sops or tariffs; ask for a better environment for everyone.** "Rather than looking for special dispensations, business has to push for improving the business environment for everyone." (S023) "India must resist special interest pleas for targeted stimulus, additional tax breaks and protections, directed credit, subventions and subsidies" (S021) Sources: 5. High.
3. **Recognise problems early; hiding them makes them bigger.** "forbearance is ostrich-like behaviour, hoping the problem will go away." (S018) "The longer the delay in dealing with the borrower’s financial distress, the greater the loss in enterprise value." (S018) Sources: 6. High.
4. **Clean up first, then grow.** "to the question of what comes first, clean up or growth, I think the answer is unambiguously “Clean up!”" (S024) Sources: 3 (S024, S026, S020). High.
5. **Growth has to be obtained the right way.** "Growth has to be obtained in the right way." (S021) "Growth is good, but growth with stability is better, especially in a poor country where so many people live at the margin." (S029) Sources: 4. High.
6. **There is no free lunch; someone always pays.** "There are no creative ways of extracting more money from the RBI – there is no free lunch!" (S029) "I have a god-given right to get a low interest rate but I won't deposit at that rate where is the money going to come from" (S006) Sources: 4. High.
7. **Default is part of risk-taking; unfair loss sharing is the problem.** "An economy where there is no default is an economy where promoters and banks are taking too little risk." (S018) "A banker who lends with the intent of never experiencing a default is probably over-conservative and will lend to too few projects, thus hurting growth." (S026) Sources: 4. High.
8. **Put real equity at risk; do not borrow your equity.** "Promoters should not try and finance mega projects with tiny slivers of equity." (S018) Sources: 3 (S018, S026, S028). High.
9. **Separate honest failure from wilful default.** "I do want to distinguish between genuine defaulters who are defaulting for business risk and willful defaulters who have whisked away money from the the company" (S006) Sources: 3 (S006, S021, S026). High.
10. **Do not throw good money after bad; doubling an unviable project does not save it.** "If the project is unviable, doubling its size does not make it any more viable." (S026) "Stalled projects do not get any better over time." (S026) Sources: 2 (S024, S026). Medium.
11. **Jobs are the test of growth.** "Job is the best form of inclusion." (S023) "a job is the best way to reduce inequality" (S005) Sources: 6 (S004, S005, S009, S012, S016, S023). High.
12. **Invest in people before things.** "If we If we don't invest in our human capital, we risk our future." (S007) Sources: 8. High.
13. **Predictable policy beats clever policy.** "I don't think we want to have volatile tax policy because that makes it hard for anybody to plan" (S004) Sources: 5. High.
14. **Decide under uncertainty, without paralysis, and accept some mistakes.** "Policy making invariably involves taking measured risks in the face of uncertainty." (S029) "I think the issue is not to allow yourself to be paralyzed by the consequences of your decisions." (S014) Sources: 3. High.
15. **Listen to everyone, then decide; do not succumb to whoever lobbies.** "you should not succumb uh to the wishes of any party because ultimately they will not thank you for that" (S006) "Producers love lower interest rate, and they do not hesitate to tell us." (S020) Sources: 3. High.
16. **Be ambitious, but do not believe you have arrived.** "it is good for India to be an ambitious country but it is bad for India to think it has reached" (S005) "it's when we start feeling nothing can go wrong that things start going wrong" (S011) Sources: 3 (S005, S006, S011). High.
17. **Test ideas on facts; nobody is beyond challenge.** "nothing should be excluded but everything should be subject to debate and constant testing. No one should be allowed to offer unquestioned pronouncements." (S022) Sources: 3 (S022, S025, S011). High.
18. **Frameworks and institutions beat personalities.** "Credibility comes from frameworks and institutions." (S020) "Any reforms have to be institutionalized, so that they outlive the reformer’s passing." (S021) Sources: 4. High.
19. **Implementation is the bottleneck; follow up.** "I always said in the RBI the big problem in India is implementation." (S005) Sources: 2 (S005, S013). Medium.
20. **Look abroad; the domestic market is not your only market.** "the domestic market is not your market look abroad and try and invest for the broader world." (S009) Sources: 1. Low.
21. **Someone must put their name on a decision.** "some senior banker ought to put her name on the proposal, taking responsibility for recommending the loan." (S028) Sources: 1. Low.
22. **Design for the customer you actually have.** "There is no point creating security for the ideal customer, instead the system should incorporate the possibility of mistakes by the actual customer that we have." (S027) Sources: 2 (S017, S027). Medium.
