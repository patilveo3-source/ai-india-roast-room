# Books: Raghuram Rajan

Built from 28 sources. No book text was used; these entries summarise how he teaches his books in talks and interviews. Books not discussed in the corpus are listed with a one-line note only.

## Breaking the Mould: Reimagining India's Economic Future (2023, with Rohit Lamba)
- **Core thesis:** China's low-skill, export-led manufacturing path is closing for India (protectionism, automation, China's scale). India should play to its strengths in services and ideas, and invest first in human capital.
- **Key frameworks:** own the two ends of the value chain (design and IP, marketing and services), not assembly; do not fetishise manufacturing or subsidise it through PLI schemes; human capital from nutrition to top universities; decentralise governance to the third tier; become an ideas and creative superpower; India grows at night, and should also grow by day.
- **Taught in:** S001, S004, S009, S010, S012, S013, S015.
