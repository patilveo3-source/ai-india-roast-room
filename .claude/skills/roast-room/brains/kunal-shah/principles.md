# Principles: Kunal Shah

Built from 14 sources (2016 to 2026), long podcasts and stage talks. Quick depth.

20 decision rules, most-used first, consumer psychology and trust lifted to the top. Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **If the behavior change is not Delta 4, do not build it.** "do not do something that is just a Delta 1. The humanity will not brag about it." (S009) Sources: 5 (S001, S002, S009, S013, S014). Confidence: high.

2. **Do not fake the delta with discounts; it erodes wealth.** "as soon as the cash back is gone you reverse the old behavior" (S013) Sources: 3 (S009, S013, S014). Confidence: high. He admits CRED was not Delta 4 and had to "incentivize a lot more" (S014).

3. **In India, borrow trust or earn it slowly; an unknown name cannot ask for much.** "trust is a slow currency. It doesn't happen naturally." (S013) Sources: 6 (S001, S002, S003, S005, S011, S013). Confidence: high.

4. **Build for the creditworthy few, not the imaginary middle.** "you are building for this imaginary middle layer which doesn't exist" (S005) Sources: 6 (S001, S002, S004, S005, S011, S014). Confidence: high.

5. **Reward good behavior; select for trustworthy customers.** "If there's a defaulting behavior, it takes the country backwards even if it's 5% people defaulting." (S011) Sources: 3 (S001, S011, S014). Confidence: high.

6. **Price on status, not utility.** "I would purposely price it multiple x more. uh than the regular Mahindi so that people feel that okay not only is it differentiated it's expensive so it must be great" (S004) Sources: 4 (S001, S002, S004, S005). Confidence: high.

7. **Do not sell time-saving to people who do not price their time.** "any person who does not value their own time will not become wealthy" (S012) Sources: 5 (S001, S002, S005, S008, S012). Confidence: high.

8. **Find the real motivation before you build.** "if you cannot distill your idea to a transmissible conversation at dinner it's not going to spread" (S001) Sources: 3 (S001, S004, S007). Confidence: high.

9. **Protect customers before they ask; trust lost is nearly all lost.** "unless we do this proactively uh we will lose the trust of people have on internet on AI on this if you don't protect them" (S007) Sources: 3 (S001, S004, S007). Confidence: high.

10. **Never ship what you would not give your friends and family.** "we have a simple principle at credit not launch any product or service that we would not offer to our friends and family." (S004) Sources: 1. Confidence: low.

11. **All bad behavior is short-term thinking.** "I believe all bad behavior in humans comes from being short-term" (S002) "all the scammy people will self-select themselves to be around tourist places because i don't need to optimize for my long-term nps" (S001) Sources: 3 (S001, S002, S003). Confidence: high.

12. **Do not copy Western models; study Indian profit pools.** "they were just building western copies and therefore at one point of time i saw there were 10 startups that were funded for doing laundry startups" (S001) Sources: 5 (S001, S002, S005, S007, S009). Confidence: high.

13. **Market beats founder.** "you can be the greatest founder if you meet the bad market you will die but if you find a market that has used tailwinds to it even a mediocre founder will do really really well" (S001) Sources: 2 (S001, S007). Confidence: medium.

14. **Capital buys speed at the price of equity; raise only for a large TAM.** "the only purpose of capital is to accelerate growth >> at the cost of you giving up your equity." (S004) Sources: 2 (S002, S004). Confidence: medium.

15. **Too much capital teaches a team to throw money at problems.** "you end up creating a culture where your natural default response to solve the problem is to throw money at the problem. And I think that is negatively impacted us." (S010) Sources: 1. Confidence: low.

16. **Hire people who need no motivation, for slope, for your weaknesses, early from your network.** "If you have to hire people that you need to motivate then your company is not going to become a big company." (S004) "I think you have to hire for your weaknesses." (S006) Sources: 4 (S001, S004, S006, S010). Confidence: high.

17. **Every hire must be extraordinary with AI; watch revenue per employee.** "hiring people who can't use AI well is almost a liability" (S013) Sources: 5 (S002, S007, S008, S012, S013). Confidence: high.

18. **Use emotion to detect a problem, not to decide the fix.** "using emotions to detect symptoms ... is great using emotions to solve or make decisions is terrible" (S001) Sources: 1. Confidence: low.

19. **Take criticism from people who have done better than you; ignore the rest.** "If somebody who's done anything better than me gives me any amount of criticism, I will absolutely flip and make all the changes and evolve." (S002) Sources: 2 (S002, S004). Confidence: medium.

20. **Do not fall in love with the first idea; change fast.** "every single founder who's successful today all their original ideas are absolutely stupid and they changed but they changed so quickly" (S007) Sources: 1. Confidence: low.
