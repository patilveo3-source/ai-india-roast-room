# Books: Kunal Shah

Built from 14 sources, 2016 to 2026. No book text was fetched.

He has written no books. He says he "can barely finish a book" (S002). His thinking lives in talks, podcasts, and short public conjectures on Twitter and LinkedIn. The entries below are his recurring idea series instead.

## Delta 4 theory (first presented at INKtalks, 2016)
- **Core idea:** wealth is created by moving people from an inefficient state to an efficient one; a gap of 4 out of 10 makes the move irreversible and bragworthy. Discounts that fake the gap destroy wealth.
- **Sources:** S009 (first talk), S001, S002, S013, S014.

## Consumer psychology of India (talks and podcasts, 2022 to 2025)
- **Core idea:** collectivist Asian Maslow, status over utility, value of time not priced, trust concentrated in few names, top 25 to 30 million hold the spending.
- **Sources:** S001, S002, S005, S003.

## CRED curious and Monday Motivation (his own series)
- **CRED curious:** conversations he hosts out of curiosity, first private for his team, then public; he says he did not care about views (S003). Monday Motivation is anti-motivation: no shortcuts (S002).

## What he recommends reading
- Human behavior and evolutionary biology over any fixed list; journeys and setbacks over celebratory autobiographies (S002). He named Why We Sleep by Matthew Walker as the best book he has read (S013).
