# Stories: Kunal Shah

Built from 14 sources, 2016 to 2026. 14 stories he reuses, with the lesson.

1. **The laundry startups.** Ten (later he says 25) laundry startups were funded in India, copied from New York, when Indian homes have maids. Lesson: no Delta 4 for Indian users; find the river before the dam (S001, S007, S009).
2. **The suitcase company.** A listed luggage maker wanted airport ads; 80 to 90 percent of its suitcases are bought for weddings, not travel. Lesson: know the real motivation (S001, S005).
3. **Living room versus bedroom.** A retail buyer found living-room goods had 3x the margin of bedroom goods; a renovation company launched "living room renovation" and it became 70 to 80 percent of revenue. Lesson: Indians pay to be seen (S001, S005).
4. **Black henna and mixtapes.** As a teenager he sold personalized mixtapes at 3 to 4x and black henna at 10x, with artificial scarcity. Lesson: people pay for personalization, difference, and status (S001, S004).
5. **Truecaller's party trick.** Nobody found Truecaller through an ad; someone showed them the trick. Lesson: UBP beats paid ads (S009).
6. **FreeCharge, 2010 to 2015.** Mobile recharge online with merchant-funded coupons; Delta 4 for youth; sold for nearly 450 million dollars. He did not know what CAC meant when Sequoia asked. Lesson: efficiency plus reward wins adoption, but a poor cohort cannot be monetized (S001, S002, S014).
7. **CRED was not Delta 4.** Card holders already paid bills online, so CRED had to pay rewards to move them. Lesson: you can buy a switch, but it costs; the bet was on the most creditworthy 25 million (S014, S005).
8. **CRED's e-commerce in a month.** CRED launched commerce and it got big in a month, surprising Western investors. Lesson: concentration of trust lets a trusted name cross-sell (S001).
9. **IPL ads in peak COVID.** Board members said no; he bet the top 30 to 40 million were home and starved of entertainment. It worked and others copied it. Lesson: judgment on limited data is the leader's job (S004).
10. **Family financial crisis.** His father's business failed; the family was thrown out of their home over a loan; he worked from 15, took philosophy because it ran 7 to 9 am. Lesson: long-term thinking; skill, reputation, trust compound (S001, S002, S003, S014).
11. **The voice-clone scam.** A parent gets a call in their child's cloned voice while the child is on a flight. CRED put capital into scam protection. Lesson: protect customers before trust breaks (S007).
12. **Explain it in Hindi.** He asks polished founders to explain their pitch in Hindi; those who understand it can switch. Lesson: real understanding survives a change of language (S001).
13. **Crocodiles, sharks, horseshoe crabs.** Asked AI what species survived 100 million years: low metabolism at will, high strike rate, adaptation. Lesson for companies in crisis (S002, S008).
14. **Cultured pearls and lab diamonds.** When pearls could be grown, their status died. Lesson: cheap copies kill the status that props up a profit pool (S002).
