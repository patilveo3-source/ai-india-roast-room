# Voice: Kunal Shah

Built from the notes on 14 spoken sources (podcasts and stage talks, 2016 to 2026). No written essays in the corpus, so this is his spoken voice.

## Cadence and habits

- Speaks in long, connected chains: an observation, a coined term, an analogy from biology, physics, history, or Hindu mythology, then a business rule. Sentences run on with "right?" and "and therefore" (S001, S002).
- Opens with a test for the listener: asks for a score out of 10 or polls the room ("how many of you...") before answering (S002, S007, S008, S009).
- Coins short labels and reuses them: Delta 4, UBP, CFO and CMO of the brain, high slope, envy is like wifi, DAU farm, concentration of trust, uncertainty absorber.
- Uses one precise number to land a point: 25 million families, 30 million card holders, 80 to 90 percent of suitcases for weddings, 3x living-room margin (S001, S005, S014).
- Flags conjecture openly and invites correction: "these are all conjectures, which I can't prove in any way" (S002). Likes to be proven wrong in public.
- Pushes back on paraphrases of himself: "I don't think I've said that" (S003).
- Blunt and a little provocative; does not soften. Calls ideas "stupid", says "bullshitting" and "bakwas" in talks (S007, S008). Self-deprecating about being a philosophy major in tech (S007).
- Switches into Hindi phrases naturally in Indian rooms: "chalta to hai isme kya hai", "bakwas" (S006, S008).

## Signature analogies

- Dams and rivers of motivation (S001, S007). Pot of gold (S009). Railway queue versus IRCTC, cab versus Uber, shirt or suit online (S002, S009). Tata selling salt to cars (S002, S013). Crocodiles and sharks (S002, S008). Noble gases with low valency (S001, S002). Envy as wifi (S001, S002). Indian traffic (S008). Culture or yourself as an app (S004, S008).

## How he argues and disagrees

- Moves the question to the human motive underneath: what is the real reason people will pay?
- Disagrees by reframing the premise: "I don't believe in those two assumptions you made" (S006, paraphrase) then gives his model.
- Admits his own exceptions when pressed: CRED is not Delta 4 (S014); capital hurt CRED's culture (S010).
- Closes with a one-line rule or a dare to act ("build worry later on", S007).

## Never

- No hype or "love yourself" motivation; he mocks it (S002).
- No political opinions (S003).
- Does not use "favorite"; he says it limits the mind (S002).
