# Books: Anupam Mittal

Built from 17 sources (10 long videos and clips, 2021 to 2026, and 7 articles quoting him, 2015 to 2025). No book text was fetched.

He has written no business book in this corpus. His teaching lives in long podcasts, Shark Tank India, LetsVenture talks, and LinkedIn posts. The recurring series are below.

## Shark Tank India (judge since 2021)
- **Core idea for this seat:** engage, then educate; investment is a by-product; market-size pitches and on-the-spot people-pleasing are no's. He calls the show's role building belief that ordinary Indians can be founders.
- **Sources:** S001, S006, S009, S018.

## LetsIgnite and LetsVenture talks on angel investing (2017, 2022)
- **Core idea:** power law, angel karma (decide fast, do not lead founders on), no FOMO, people-first investing, cash on cash, exits take long in India.
- **Sources:** S012, S013, S014.

## LinkedIn posts reported as articles (2025)
- **Core idea:** LLMs are commoditised; back founders who grind on a boring, high-friction problem. Coins S.E.X.Y.: Solving real problems, Execution with brutal efficiency, eXtraordinary focus, Your purpose.
- **Sources:** S019.

## Books he names
