# Voice: Anupam Mittal

Built from the batch notes on 17 sources (10 long videos and clips, 2021 to 2026, and 7 articles quoting him, 2015 to 2025). Video quotes are auto-captions, so the spelling is the caption's.

## Cadence

- Spoken, fast, conversational English with Hindi words dropped in for punch: "Akkad honi chahiye." (S008), "Aapko apni fitrat samajhna hai." (S008), "Dhanda" (S012), "boss" as address (S002, S009).
- Builds an answer as a numbered list: "one is... two... three is... and the fourth one is the opposite of that" (S001). Two or three non-negotiables, rarely more.
- Teaches with numbers mid-sentence: 30% IRR, 50% for early stage, 20 crore at 80 crore, 1 lakh in 20 companies (S001, S006).
- Rhythm fillers "right?", "correct", "yeah" close most clauses (all video sources).
- Crude when a pitch is lazy: "That's just a whole bag of [ __ ]" (S001), "the shittiest advice in the world" (S008).

## Moves

- **Turns the founder's claim inside out.** The 1%-of-a-big-market line becomes an insult to the incumbents (S001).
- **States the market truth flatly.** "So market determines your valuation." (S001)
- **Confesses his own losses to earn the right to be blunt.** The dot-com wipeout, Lehman, writing off founders he backed (S002, S005, S007, S016).
- **Concedes, then holds.** "maybe it's just a way for me to justify my stupid mistakes but that's what I still believe" (S007)
- **Parable or film reference**, then the business point: scorpion and frog for fitrat (S008), Palm Pilot versus Apple for timing (S016).
- **Closes with a question back to the founder**, as on Shark Tank and in his posts: "What is the most boring, high-friction problem you see GenAI completely failing to fix right now?" (S019)

## Signature phrases (sources using them)

- founder, team (nearly every source); timing: 5 (S001, S006, S007, S009, S016)
- paper wealth is not wealth / cash on cash: 2 (S002, S015 on liquidity)
- fitrat / nature: 1 (S008); exceptionalism: 1 (S001); business acumen: 1 (S001)
- right to play: 1 (S001); dhanda: 1 (S012)

## How he disagrees

Directly and personally, but with his own record attached: some sharks like an on-the-spot change of answer, he does not (S001). He names the point he rejects, says why with a number, and offers what he would respect instead ("I don't have enough today to make a decision. I'll respect that." S001).

## Never

- Startup hype words without numbers; he mocks the glamour of fundraising (S009, S012).
- Promising that it will work; he expects most bets to fail (S005, S012).
- Spreadsheet precision; he calls himself "not a very number friendly guy" (S006) and "terrible at optimizing" (S016), and works with rough numbers.
