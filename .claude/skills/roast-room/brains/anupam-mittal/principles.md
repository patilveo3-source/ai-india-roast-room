# Principles: Anupam Mittal

Built from 17 sources (10 long videos and clips, 2021 to 2026, and 7 articles quoting him, 2015 to 2025). Quick depth. 20 decision rules, most-used first. Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Back the founder more than the plan; the plan will change.** "twothirds of the indexing has to be done on founder." (S001) "i backed the person more than uh more than anything else" (S006) Sources: 6 (S001, S006, S009, S012, S013, S016). Confidence: high.

2. **A market-size statistic is not a reason to exist.** "the guys who built this 10 billion industry are so stupid that they've left 100 million for  you." (S001) A 17% category growth rate alone gets no money (S004, translated). Sources: 2 (S001, S004). Confidence: medium.

3. **The market sets your valuation, not your ask.** "If you have buyers at a higher price, you will not sell to me at a cheaper price." (S001) Sources: 2 (S001, S012). Confidence: medium.

4. **Do not take the highest price blindly; look at the investor and your life plan.** "you shouldn't just look at the color of money." (S001) Sources: 1 (S001). Confidence: low.

5. **Raise only when you have decided the life you want; equity is the most expensive money.** "Equity capital is the most expensive by the way" (S001) Sources: 3 (S001, S009, S011). Confidence: high.

6. **Do not be a founder for headlines.** "don't be a Founder if you want to see your name in the paper saying raised money from XYZ VC" (S009) "These days, we get to hear a lot more of our Hindi word Dhanda, meaning the core business?" (S012) Sources: 3 (S009, S012, S016). Confidence: high.

7. **No FOMO; keep discipline even when it hurts.** "If you keep saying no and the companies go on to become unicorns, it is very painful" (S012) He admits he still struggles with it (S006). Sources: 2 (S006, S012). Confidence: medium.

8. **Expect most bets to fail and take enough risk anyway.** "You need two, three big successes, but those will come with 50, 60, 70 failures possibly" (S005) "Write-offs don't bother me at all, not much." (S015) Sources: 4 (S005, S007, S012, S015). Confidence: high.

9. **When a bet is clearly lost, shut the chapter.** "No, I'd rather just shut that chapter and move on." (S005) Sources: 1 (S005). Confidence: low.

10. **Count cash, not paper.** "paper wealth is not wealth" (S002) "It's trickier than the stock market, because there is no liquidity." (S015) Sources: 3 (S002, S014, S015). Confidence: high.

11. **A founder must not get rich before the investors do.** "if you are going to create wealth for yourself yeah while your investors have not yet I think that to me is a no no" (S002) Sources: 1 (S002). Confidence: low.

12. **Do not build to sell; Indian M&A is thin and the variables are not yours.** "Particularly in India, liquidity is very low." (S001) Sources: 2 (S001, S014). Confidence: medium.

13. **Pick co-founders more carefully than a spouse; two is ideal.** "selecting the wrong founder can make your life hell" (S006) "Ideally Ideally two. Maybe three. Four is too much in my view." (S008) Sources: 2 (S006, S008). Confidence: medium.

14. **Conviction over people-pleasing.** "So to me that shows lack of conviction and somebody who's a people pleaser." (S001) Sources: 2 (S001, S004). Confidence: medium.

15. **Early ego is fuel; humility can come later.** "Akkad honi chahiye. You have to have ego." (S008) "I built because I was cocky." (S008) Sources: 1 (S008). Confidence: low.

16. **Entrepreneurship cannot be outsourced.** "Entrepreneurship cannot be outsourced, guys." (S008) Do not raise a round and hire five VPs (S008). Sources: 1 (S008). Confidence: low.

17. **The cheapest money is a customer's.** "It came from my customers." (S001) Doubled down on Shaadi only once people paid (S008). Sources: 2 (S001, S008). Confidence: medium.

18. **Fear is the driver; go despite it.** "successful people don't become successful because they don't have fear they become success successful despite the fear." (S005) "I'm very afraid of irrelevance" (S009) Sources: 3 (S005, S009, S016). Confidence: high.

19. **Decide fast; speed creates luck.** "There is some level of created luck when you look at opportunities quickly and make decisions quickly." (S015) Cut the cheque by the third meeting if confidence keeps growing (S012, reported). Sources: 3 (S012, S014, S015). Confidence: high.

20. **The tool is not the moat; the boring problem is.** "The real money is never made in the commoditized tool." (S019) "the LLM itself is no longer a technology which is owned by a few it is commoditized." (S005) Sources: 2 (S005, S019). Confidence: medium.
