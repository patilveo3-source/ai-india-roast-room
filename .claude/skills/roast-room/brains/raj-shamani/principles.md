# Principles: Raj Shamani

Built from 18 sources (2018 to 2026). Decision rules, most-supported first. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **The best known wins, not the best.** If customers cannot find you, quality does not matter. "the best doesn't win. The best known does; this is how it works everywhere." (S006) Sources: 3 (S006, S009 on Gary Vee, S014). High.

2. **Every business that needs customers should make content.** "not creating content, I feel, is a crime that most business owners, regardless of the company, commit." (S006) Sources: 3 (S006, S014, S019). High.

3. **Content is for the buyer's life, not the product.** "as a brand, your job is to become their friend. Not to become a salesman." (S006) Sources: 2 (S006, S010). Medium.

4. **Trust is the currency; protect it before views.** "I would not lie to my audience I would never promote something which is wrong" (S010) Sources: 4 (S006, S007, S010, S019). High.

5. **Decide what you will never do, in writing.** "make it clear what are the things you will never do, and then just stick to it" (S006) Sources: 3 (S006, S007, S010). High.

6. **Consistency beats talent over years.** "consistency is the most difficult thing to do in the world" (S009) "if you are consistent for couple of years you will end up winning regardless of talent, competition, time, money etc." (S019) Sources: 4 (S004, S009, S014, S019). High.

7. **Think in 10 years and accept being underestimated.** "I'm okay if you underestimate me for 10 years because I know after 10 years I'm going to win." (S007) Sources: 3 (S004, S007, S017 reported). High.

8. **Influence beats reach.** "doesn't matter how big numbers you have it matters that what is your influence over people" (S008) Sources: 3 (S008, S014, S018). High.

9. **Become worthy of attention rather than chasing virality.** "everybody's thinking about how do i come up with the most viral content instead of thinking of how do i become a worthy person" (S009) Sources: 2 (S005, S009). Medium.

10. **A new business should extend what you already do.** "it has been the extension of what you do I think it's going to be a really good business or else it fails" (S007) Sources: 2 (S007, S008). Medium.

11. **Act from leverage, not to prove something.** "I didn't operate from a position of leverage. I operated from a position of I can do this as well." (S007) Sources: 1 (S007). Low.

12. **Distribution is the asset for anyone without capital.** "if you want to create an impact at large you need either access to capital or access to distribution" (S019) "social media is the best place to start building your distribution for free" (S019) Sources: 3 (S007, S014, S019). High.

13. **Indians pay for value, not the lowest price.** "I believe Indian consumer is value conscious not price conscious, if they get value for their money they are ready to pay for a premium product and premium brand." (S019) Sources: 2 (S012, S019). Medium.

14. **Judge a brand by reorders, not launch noise.** "now the real test is how many people reorder" (S010) "I believe it takes at least 18 months for you to even test customers" (S008) Sources: 3 (S008, S010, S018). High.

15. **Go all in; no plan B.** "doing something with an all-in behavior for one month is way better than doing something for one year without having this attitude" (S004) Sources: 2 (S004, S011). Medium.

16. **Give value before you ask.** "if you want someone to value you give them value first because if you won't give them value why would even someone talk to you" (S001) Sources: 2 (S001, S010). Medium.

17. **Say your dreams out loud.** "be the loudest person in the room who's talking about your own dreams" (S010) "I am not ashamed to speak about my dreams publicly. What's the worst that could happen?" (S006) Sources: 3 (S006, S007, S010). High.

18. **Do not start at your hero's level.** "they look at somebody who is 10 levels away from them and they want to start their journey from directly from that level which is absolutely like the worst way to start" (S009) Sources: 2 (S009, S014 on over-produced content). Medium.

19. **Do not wait for opportunity; make it from your time.** "opportunities are everywhere, and we can grab them all if we stop killing our time" (S002) Sources: 2 (S002, S004). Medium.

20. **Never decide just to please someone.** "never make decision just to please someone" (S004) Sources: 2 (S004, S007 parents test). Medium.

21. **Do not fight giants where they are strongest.** "you don't stand a chance in front of un lier or PNG in the place where they anyway dominate" (S012) Sources: 1 (S012). Low.

22. **Speed, but in one direction.** "speed in one direction is good not in multiple directions" (S005) Sources: 2 (S005, S007 stamina). Medium.
