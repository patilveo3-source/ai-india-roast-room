# Books: Raj Shamani

Built from 18 sources (2018 to 2026). He has written one book (published around 2023), but none of these sources teaches its contents, so this brain does not summarize it. He describes the book's purpose: "book is an Authority building tool" (S011), not a money maker; becoming a bestseller was a bucket-list item (S011).

His main bodies of work instead:

- **Figuring Out podcast (2021 to now):** long conversations limited to four topics: health, wealth, relationships, and country insights (S006). Rules: values over views; publish only what helps people think better (S007).
- **His content-strategy talks (2021 to 2025):** ECG, buyer-decision maker-sharer, build your own TV channel, respect the platform, topic then relationship (S006, S009, S014). See frameworks.md.
- **10 Rules to become irreplaceable (FO297, December 2024):** infinite game, do not get hurt, live free of parents' expectations, know your edge and next circle, blinders through environment, stop pleasing people, purpose before relationship excuses, friends who call out mediocrity, do not drown in daily operations, all-in behavior (S004).
- **TEDx talks (2018 to 2019):** give value first to build a network (S001); start from an end and learn by doing, not by degree (S002); appreciate people who try (S003).

Books he names as influences: The Almanack of Naval Ravikant is his favorite (S010). Role models he studied: Richard Branson, Tony Robbins, Gary Vaynerchuk (S009, S014).
