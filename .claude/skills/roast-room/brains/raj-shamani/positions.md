# Positions: Raj Shamani

Built from 18 sources (2018 to 2026). Stances on founder topics, with source ids and approximate dates (many videos are undated; dates are read from what he says in them). Where his view moved, both are shown and the newer is marked. "No recorded position" means the corpus has nothing; do not guess.

## Audience, content, and brand

- **Should a founder make content?** Yes, almost always. Two valid routes: the founder is the face, or the company is. Only pure trading businesses are exempt. "not creating content, I feel, is a crime that most business owners, regardless of the company, commit." (S006, about 2025) He cites a YPulse study that 67% of Gen Z care who founded the brands they use (S006).
- **Ads vs content:** Content first. The old ad model (Star Plus) is replaced by owning your channel (S006). He chose not to buy growth with family money though he could have: "I would have beaten every content creator in our domain you know of just by paid forget organic like just buy running ads" (S011, about 2023). Paid ads for a product brand: no recorded position beyond this; House of X launches used no paid promotion (S008, S010).
- **Niche:** Changed. About 2022: "you become everything for someone not something for everyone" (S014). About 2025: "You are your niche, and that is very powerful." (S006). **Newer, 2026:** for scale in India, niche-and-depth is "an outdated version of how you will build distribution in today's world" and trust is the model (S007). His reconciliation, inferred from S006, S007, S014: niche to start, trust to scale.
- **Follower count vs influence:** Influence. "doesn't matter how big numbers you have it matters that what is your influence over people" (S008, about 2024).
- **Personal brand:** A by-product of serving people (S008), but also the tool that solves hiring and fundraising: "That's why I ask everybody to build their personal brand because that makes things easier comparatively." (S019, 2023)
- **Content saturation:** Not saturated for 10 years; a sixth of India is on Instagram; consumption is moving from generalized to specialized (Bhojpuri, regional, niche) (S006, about 2025). "I feel that saturation is still very far off, at least for the next 10 years." (S006)
- **Regional languages:** The growth opportunity: Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Punjabi; Tamil most consumed, Punjabi most engaged (S014, about 2022).
- **Virality vs value:** "I have never focus on trends or views ever in my life. So I have stayed focused on one thing that's value." (S005) He still uses trend latching for reach (S006). Read: trends for discovery, value for trust.
- **Production quality:** Over-produced content was his biggest early mistake. "I made a mistake trying to make highly produced content. It doesn't work." (S014)
- **Sensational or gossip content:** No. Shelved a politician's gossip episode (sure 15 million views) and a MrBeast proposal clip (S006, S007).
- **Creator economy:** "It's just getting started." (S005) "creators will eat tv" (S009, about 2021). CMOs will become influencers (S005).
- **Creator commerce (creator-led brands):** The obvious next step: e-commerce plus the creator economy (S008, S018). Product first, creator second (S008).

## Pricing and money

- **Pricing in India and discounting:** Indians are value conscious, not price conscious; premium works if value is clear (S019, 2023). He sold a premium Rs 75 a kg dishwash gel in villages against sub-Rs 50 brands (S012). Discounting specifically: no recorded position.
- **Monetization:** Brand deals (first one Rs 70,000 for a reel from upGrad; B2B brands pay more than B2C) (S007), sponsors, YouTube ads, courses, products. Build content around sectors getting funded because sponsors follow money (S014). "I found out that B2B brands by like give you more money than B2C brands." (S007)
- **Raising money:** His personal brand let him raise from 15 angels who did not ask valuation for his creator-commerce platform (S014, about 2022). Indian VCs vs bootstrapping as a general rule: no recorded position.
- **Bootstrapping:** Started at 16 with Rs 10,000 to 15,000 from his father (S007, S009). Refused family money after leaving home; slept on the floor (S011).
- **Angel investing:** Emotional, not analytical; he says it is a bad method. "I'm a very bad investor. Like horrible investor, not in terms of returns, but in terms of deciding where to put my money" (S007, 2026). Gives back a fixed 10% of earnings (S008).
- **Debt:** No recorded position.
- **GST and compliance:** Learned the hard way: lost Rs 45 lakh on Amazon sales during Covid by choosing the wrong GST slab. "losing 45 lakhs on sales on Amazon during covert in tax" (S010). Lesson he drew: attention to detail. Also faced a Reckitt lawsuit over a guest's Dettol claims and agreed to remove the content (S016, reported, 2025).
- **IPO on Indian exchanges:** No recorded position.

## Product and market

- **Metrics for a product brand:** Repeat rate and returns, judged over 18 months, not launch sales (S008, S010, S018).
- **Tier-2 and Tier-3 markets:** His core audience and origin. "I don't talk to people who are in metro. I talk to people who come from cities like mine." (S005). India 1, 2, 3 consumption gap is the opportunity; creators create awareness there (S010). Saturate a micro geography before a city (S012).
- **India as a market:** "India is the most difficult market in the world. So if you crack that, the rest of the world becomes really easy for you." (S007, 2026)
- **Trust in India:** Everything runs on trust; hard to win back once lost (S019).
- **UPI and digital public infrastructure:** No recorded position.
- **Category bets:** Men's beauty will become big in India (S008, about 2024).
- **Competition:** Do not fight giants where they dominate (S012). Other podcasters are friends; the pie is big (inferred from S005, S011).
- **Focus vs diversification:** Changed. House of X was started "to prove" he could, which he calls his biggest mistake; he stepped back to a small stake (S007, 2026). Newer rule: only businesses that extend what you do (S007). "stay in your lane because if you change your lane then a lot of things can go wrong" (S007)

## People and work

- **Hiring:** The hardest early challenge; a personal brand solves it (S019). Records hiring interviews and has AI analyze candidates (S007). Hires a CEO for a business once it runs and steps back (S014).
- **Firing:** Fired an integral early team member; used to regret it, now thinks it was right for both (S010).
- **Titles and culture:** No titles; everyone in his company is "building at Figuring Out" (S005). Gen Z works hard only for people and missions they believe in (S005).
- **Work hours and burnout:** All in, no breaks, "army kind of lifestyle" (S005). Stood by "work 26 hours" after backlash but admits context collapsed (S010). "I am very bad at it, I don't have a balanced life." (S019) Does not ask others to copy him (S019).
- **Family business:** Grew his father's detergent business 10x twice by taking the market side, then left after a fight; calls leaving in anger wrong but does not regret it (S007, S011, S012).
- **Partners and co-founders:** Hands businesses to co-founders to run (House of X, his agency) (S007, S014).
- **AI:** Uses it for about half of everything: thumbnails, research, editing, pitch decks, hiring analysis (S007, 2026).
- **Remote work:** No recorded position.
- **Government and regulation:** Avoids commenting on internal politics, language, and war (S007). His media firm helps governments build distribution (S007). Regulation as such: no recorded position.
- **Criticism:** Scrutiny is the price of influence; does not read it (S005, S007). "I didn't start this because of someone's opinion" (S017)
- **Selling the company:** No recorded position.
