# Stories: Raj Shamani

Built from 18 sources (2018 to 2026). The stories he reuses, with the lesson he draws.

1. **The Rs 15,000 start.** At 16, after his father's diabetic attack, he applied to 70 to 80 FMCG companies, heard nothing, and joined his father, who gave him Rs 10,000 to 15,000 to sell, market, or hire. Lesson: constraint teaches. "Those 15,000 taught me everything that I know today." (S007) Also S009, S012, S019.

2. **Expensive detergent for expensive clothes.** Sold a Rs 75 a kg dishwash gel in villages against sub-Rs 50 brands, built 200 distributors, grew 10x in 18 months and 10x again. Lesson: premium sells in small towns if you explain the value (S012).

3. **Autorickshaws and no-parking boards.** Repaired rickshaws for free in exchange for painting them as Jadugar ads; used no-parking boards on bungalows. Lesson: find media nobody prices as media (S012).

4. **Standing in Big Bazaar.** At 17 he stood for months in Big Bazaar watching shoppers move from soaps to liquids, and picked dishwash gel because only two brands existed. Lesson: watch transitions in modern retail (S012).

5. **The pani puri delegate.** At a free networking event he joined as "founder" of his surname's company, nobody talked to him until he offered a hungry delegate pani puri; the man ran a big youth organization and later sent him an offer to work with leaders from 30 countries. Lesson: give value first (S001).

6. **Nobody bought the agency.** Pitched friends and their parents a Gary Vee style branding agency; nobody bought ("You have no credibility"), so he became his own client and built the proof first (S006, S007, S014).

7. **Gary Vee in Singapore, 2018.** As a filler speaker he asked only to watch how Gary handled people; Gary told him to document his journey, not claim expertise. That became the name Figuring Out (S009, S010).

8. **The pandemic sprint.** Three videos a day, 700 to 800 in six months, no missed day from June 2020. Lesson: consistency is the moat for someone with no credential (S009, S014).

9. **54 deleted episodes.** His first 54 podcast episodes reached 50 listeners in total; he deleted them; an earlier show, Voices with Raj (2017), was shut down; for the next one, 400 asks got one yes (S006, S017).

10. **The politician he never uploaded.** A politician gave him pure gossip worth at least 15 million views; he shelved it and his team was threatened. He also shelved a superstar's episode after the star's team abused his staff, and a MrBeast proposal clip (S006, S007). Lesson: values over views.

11. **Vijay Mallya.** Met him by chance in London, spent six months convincing him, published four hours that drew 20 million views in four days and heavy criticism. He stands by it: "There has to be a podcast which teaches you what not to do." (S007)

12. **House of X.** Launched creator brands with King (Blanko perfume) and chef Sanjyot Keer; strong launch with zero marketing and above-norm repeat rates (S008, S010). By 2026 he calls starting it from a need to prove himself his biggest mistake and has stepped back to a small stake (S007).

13. **The Rs 45 lakh GST slab.** Chose the wrong GST slab on Amazon sales during Covid and lost Rs 45 lakh. Lesson: attention to detail (S010).

14. **Sleeping on the floor in Mumbai.** Left the family business after a fight with his father, refused family money, and slept on the floor until the next brand deal paid for a bed (S011). Moved to Mumbai on January 3, 2021 against his parents' wishes (S010).

15. **The credit card for his mother.** His most rewarding moment: gave his mother a credit card and told her never to look at the bill (S010, S019).

16. **"Jai Hind" at the UN.** Froze in his first UN speech, blurted "Jai Hind", the room laughed and clapped, and he recovered. Speaking had started as free marketing for the detergent (S007, S010).
