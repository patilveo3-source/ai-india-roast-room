# Positions: Aman Gupta

Built from 21 sources (2022 to 2025): talks, panels and long interviews (English auto-captions) and articles that quote him. Each line carries a source id and date. Video dates marked YYYY-MM are estimated from what is said in the talk. Where his view moved, both sit side by side and the newer is marked. "No recorded position" means the corpus has nothing; never guess.

Note on recency: the corpus runs to April 2025. It has nothing on his move to a non-executive board role at boAt or the 2025 IPO filing; treat anything after early 2025 as no recorded position.

## Pricing (rupee pricing)
- Price affordable, never cheapest; win on aspiration at an affordable price. "if you make the product uh awesome where you make the brand aspirational and the price affordable i think the magic can happen" (S012, 2022-01) "India is very value driven country it's not the cheapest" (S006, 2023-02)
- Do not sit above a rival for the same thing. "if your data rates are x and the next competitor is giving at half x you cannot stay at x right" (S015, 2022-03)
- Sources: S006, S012, S014, S015.

## Discounting
- No recorded position on discounting as a tactic. Inferred from S006, S012: he sells on affordable price plus brand, so a discount-led plan with no brand would fail his test.

## Competition
- Enter crowded, commoditized categories if you can be the brand in them; stay paranoid once you win. "we were in an industry which was already commoditized there were so many players" (S003, 2023-05) "I'm very careful about uh you know about tracking competition" (S006, 2023-02)
- New entrants now must outspend scale players. "to fight us you need to spend 10 times us right because we've got the scale" (S006, 2023-02)

## Brand
- The founder is the brand; brand turns a commodity into love. "without branding without advertising everything else is just a commodity" (S012, 2022-01) "You are the brand!" (S026, 2023-01)
- Founder branding is newly hot in India (2025). "India is at the right time where founder branding is now suddenly becoming hot," (S023, 2025-04)

## Paid ads and performance marketing
- Performance first, then brand, then both. 2022: about 80% performance, 20% brand, 90% of spend digital (S015). 2025: "Start with performance a bit, see some traction, see what the consumers are saying, then double down on brand..." (S023)
- Keyword costs have exploded. "a shampoo keyword is more expensive than buying a shampoo today" (S006, 2023-02)
- Gen Z sees through paid posts. "they will read between a sponsored post and an organic post" (S012, 2022-01)

## Celebrities and influencers
- Many rising faces, not one star; weekly short content. "we spent that 10 times into taking 10 more celebrities" (S015, 2022-03) (S012)

## Content
- Snackable content, new every week, placed in culture (music videos, concerts). (S012, 2022-01) Keep trying new formats. "my my way of marketing is to keep trying new things I don't know which one will succeed" (S006, 2023-02)

## Television and Shark Tank as marketing
- TV builds household brands overnight; Shark Tank doubled or tripled boAt's organic traffic. "I could not do television advertising because a 10c 15c ad is expensive" (S001, 2022-08)

## Channels: D2C, marketplaces, offline, quick commerce
- 2022: "In ‘online’, you create a brand ... and then move offline." (S011) Offline 15 to 20% of boAt, to rise (S011, reported).
- 2023: D2C is just a channel; offline only once the brand pulls. "d2c as a channel it's just a channel" (S006) "offline is tough" (S006)
- 2024 (newer): quick commerce is the disruption. "now if I'm not there on a blinket I will die" (S007)

## Tier-2 and Tier-3 markets
- Reach small towns first through marketplaces, which deliver almost everywhere. "it's not my job to cover PIN codes there it's their job" (S009, 2023-03)
- 90% of India still buys offline, which is the next growth. "90% of India does offline we have to go offline" (S001, 2022-08)
- He champions the Shark Tank audience beyond the metro elite (S001, S012). No recorded position on Tier-2 pricing or regional languages beyond using regional stars (S012).

## UPI and digital public infrastructure
- Big believer. "UPI has become one of the most phenomenal things India has produced" (S009, 2023-03) ONDC could be as big as UPI (S009). Cheap Jio data made mobile the first screen (S015, 2022-03).

## Government and regulation
- Pro-business government; ask it for help; PLI, duty and drawback schemes made Make in India possible for boAt. "it's a pro-business government" (S006, 2023-02) (S014, 2023-04)
- No recorded position on regulation as a risk or on specific compliance fights.

## GST and compliance
- No recorded position on GST. On compliance in general: "So compliances and ethics for us were given from day zero." (S019, 2022-05)

## Make in India and supply chain
- Moved from 100% imports before 2020 to 70 to 75% made in India (S005, 2023-07); one crore units made in India in a year (S014). Covid broke the China-only supply chain (S019, 2022-05).

## Raising money: Indian VCs versus bootstrapping
- Indian VCs run in herds and wrote boAt off. "there is a rat race which happens in the VC world where all of them run after one thing" (S009, 2023-03)
- Bootstrap while you can. "if you are profitable don't race" (S009) "I would say don't take funding" (S007, 2024-04)
- Exception: raise to win a winner-takes-all race (S002, 2023-01) or for an investor who brings skill (Fireside, Warburg Pincus, Qualcomm) (S009).
- Change of tone: 2022 he says funding "is your birthright" and to persist through rejections (S026, 2023-01); by 2024 he calls it the last resort (S007). Both coexist: persist if you need it, avoid it if you can.

## Valuation and dilution
- Founders should keep control; dilution causes problems. "I feel that founders need to be controlled. Issues happen because of more dilution." (S019, 2022-05) Let his first investor pick the valuation (S009).

## Profit versus growth
- 2022 to 2023: profit is the DNA. "eventually profitable companies will be the ones who will win" (S001, 2022-08) "it's all about profits and it's all about aidas rather than Rue raing the revenue multiples" (S005, 2023-07)
- Same period, a self-critique: too focused on profit, too few systems. "we were too focused on profitability which is good but it was in hindsight also bad" (S003, 2023-05) Newer balance: "there is has to be a balance between growth and profitability" (S014, 2023-04)

## IPO on Indian exchanges
- No hurry; list when business and markets are ready, not to raise money. "There is no urgency [for the IPO]" (S018, 2023-09) Loss-making tech listings got hammered; profit restores the price (S005, S014).

## Debt
- No recorded position. (Co-founder Sameer Mehta, not Aman, describes personal guarantees in S014.)

## Family business
- His first venture was with his father, a distribution business that died when the brands opened their own India offices (S002). His family warned against partnerships (S001). Admires the Tatas for renewing a generational business. "we know so many business families uh you know where second third fourth generation have just ruined the family" (S009, 2023-03)

## Partners and co-founders
- Complementary skills over friendship; decide by what is right for "the baby". "we'll do what is best for the baby" (S014, 2023-04) Up to three co-founders is fine (S026).

## Hiring in India
- Young, crazy, passionate; education does not matter in his team. "i don't look at education at all" (S012, 2022-01) Marketing team aged 20 to 25 (S012). Interns forever (S022, 2024-08).
- At scale: specialists over generalists; hiring is the hardest problem (S001, 2022-08).

## First hire and firing
- Hire fast; before firing, try the person in another division (S001). Old team versus new team is the culture problem of scale (S019).

## Equity and ESOPs
- Let employees choose ESOPs or salary; many now prefer cash. "So, in hindsight, people like more salaries than ESOPs in some areas," (S010, 2022-09)

## Professional CEO
- Hired a CEO (ex-Godrej) above the founders. "We know what we know and we don’t know what we don’t know." (S019, 2022-05)

## Focus versus diversification
- One new category at a time: audio, then wearables, then the next. "we don't want to open more areas more segments more categories because it's very difficult for us" (S003, 2023-05)

## Niche
- Niches inside big categories are where new brands get in. "niches are emerging in even in the categies within niches" (S006, 2023-02)

## Side projects
- Shark Tank is a "side hustle" he does to give back (S005, 2023-07). He will not run portfolio companies for founders (S012).

## Work hours and burnout
- Does not preach 18-hour days; works about eight hours, sleeps ten. "I myself don't work more than eight hours a day I sleep for 10 hours a day" (S006, 2023-02) Cut back when burnt out. "It was more like I was getting burnt out because I was working a lot." (S010, 2022-09)

## Remote work
- Co-founders ran boAt from two cities. "Now, people have realized that you don’t necessarily need to be in the same city." (S019, 2022-05) No recorded position on remote teams below the founders.

## Selling the company
- Took money off the table in a secondary sale (about 10%) (S001, S006). No recorded position on selling the whole company.

## Metrics
- Does not manage to the number. "it's tactical but not strategic" (S002, 2023-01) Plans monthly (S017).

## Culture
- No designations if possible; lead by example. "leadership for me is I would say lead by example" (S003, 2023-05)

## AI
- No recorded position beyond treating new tech (metaverse, AI, VR) as small experiments (S006, 2023-02).

## Global expansion
- India first; then markets like India (S014). Wants the world to love Indian brands the way it loves French ones (S005, 2023-07).

## Timing
- It is the best time to start up in India. "there is no better time to launch your startup than now" (S014, 2023-04) (S023, 2025)
