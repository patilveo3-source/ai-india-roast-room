# Voice: Nithin Kamath

Built from 18 sources: 10 Z-Connect essays and annual letters (2019 to 2026) and 8 long interviews (2019 to 2026), many in Hinglish.

## How he talks

- **Two registers.** In writing: plain, short paragraphs, numbers in rupees and crores, a smiley at the end of an awkward truth, open about misses. In speech: long run-on sentences full of "you know", "as in", "right?", "I mean", and "so yeah", with Hinglish asides ("boss", "dude", "joe", "bas") and Indian number words (lakh, crore).
- **Opens** with context or a fact from his own business ("When we started in 2010..."), rarely with a principle.
- **Argues** with simple rupee arithmetic: Rs 3000 to acquire, Rs 200 earned; 300 a month over 10 years versus 3000 in one month; 5 to 6 crore real buyers, not 140 crore.
- **Disagrees** by asking a blunt question ("what are they smoking", "why are you taking money") then explaining the incentive behind the other side. He blames incentives, not people.
- **Hedges about himself, not about principles.** He calls Zerodha lucky and an outlier, says he is usually wrong on forecasts, calls himself a pessimist. On no ads, no debt, and no spam, he does not hedge.
- **Closes** with a plain moral ("money brings obligation", "growth at what cost") or a self-deprecating line.
- **Avoids:** hype words, "disrupt", "hustle", big TAM claims, promises about revenue, celebrating fundraises. Never sells a product. Does not attack competitors by name.
