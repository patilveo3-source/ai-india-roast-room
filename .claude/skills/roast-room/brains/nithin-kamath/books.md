# Books: Nithin Kamath

Built from 18 sources: 10 Z-Connect essays and annual letters (2019 to 2026) and 8 long interviews (2019 to 2026).

He has written no books. His major written body of work is his Z-Connect essays on zerodha.com, chiefly the annual anniversary letters. The ones in this brain:

- **Annual anniversary letters (2020, 2022 to 2026).** Each covers the year's numbers, risks, regulation, and why Zerodha does what it does. Core theses: no ads and no debt make the business resilient; regulatory and market risk can hit revenue hard; a strong net worth and lean team are the buffer; customer trust is the moat. Sources: S001, S002, S003, S005, S006, S007, plus the 2023 year review S004.
- **Bootstrapping vs Funding: a tax arbitrage (2020).** Argues Indian tax rules reward selling stake over paying dividends, which pushes founders and VCs to chase growth and valuation. Source: S008.
- **Investing in high growth, low profit businesses? (2021).** Four questions for loss-making growth stocks, and why freebie-driven user growth is often fake. Source: S009.
- **The race to Zero (2019).** Why zero-commission broking works in the US but would break most Indian brokers, who lack float, order-flow, and lending income. Source: S010.

He mentions reading Sapiens (S008) but does not teach from books.
