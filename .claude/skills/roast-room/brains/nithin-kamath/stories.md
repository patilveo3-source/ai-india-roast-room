# Stories: Nithin Kamath

Built from 18 sources: 10 Z-Connect essays and annual letters (2019 to 2026) and 8 long interviews (2019 to 2026). The stories he reuses, with the lesson he draws.

1. **Rs 10 lakh is all the money that ever went in.** Rs 3 lakh for a website and Rs 7 lakh for office computers in 2010; nothing since. Lesson: start with what you have and grow from profit. "we spent three lakhs for a website and 7 lakh rupees for computers for the office." (S011)
2. **Nikhil's trading funded the runway.** After paying about Rs 1 crore exchange deposit, the little left was traded by Nikhil to keep the firm alive until broking made money in year two or three. Lesson: find a cash source that is not investors. "The plan back in 2010 was for Nik to trade with the very little money we had left after starting Zerodha to generate enough runway for the retail broking attempt." (S007) (also S014)
3. **Free back office, then years of tech debt.** They took the back office a vendor gave free; replacing it in 2017 to 2018 slowed them for two or three years. Lesson: cheap early choices cost later; do customer-facing first anyway. "we started this business with zero rupees on technology" (S016) (also S014)
4. **Negative CAC.** Asked by startup people about CAC, he said minus 500, because Zerodha charged an account opening fee. Lesson: make the customer pay to start, and the business is never pressured to recover cost by pushing him. "they asked me what is CAC and I said minus 500 rupees" (S011) (also S012, S017)
5. **Hating the phone call.** Managing other people's money before 2010, he hated calls he had to take because they had given him money, and returned it all. Lesson: money brings obligation; freedom is the ability not to pick up. "I used to really hate the obligation money brought on the table" (S012) (also S013, S018)
6. **The 2013 startup meetings.** When Zerodha turned profitable he met VCs and founders, saw that once you raise you keep raising, and decided not to. Lesson: the deepest pocket wins a funded race. "then you know it becomes the deepest pocket wins game" (S012) (also S014)
7. **Zero brokerage on delivery, decided at an airport.** In December 2015, delivery was only 2 to 3 percent of revenue, so he went to zero; virality followed. Lesson: give away what costs you little and builds trust. "once we did that the virality kicked in" (S016) (also S012, S013)
8. **The sales team that made no difference.** Zerodha had callers chasing half-finished signups, moved them all to support, and onboarding did not drop. Lesson: pushy selling adds less than you think. "Interestingly, it didn't make any difference to the customer onboarding rates." (S006)
9. **Two million took ten years; then 2020.** First 60,000 customers took six years; first 2 million took ten; then 2 to 10 million in about two years in the COVID bull market. Lesson: luck and timing matter; be ready when the market comes. "The first 2 million took us 10 years." (S009) (also S013, S014, S015)
10. **The competitor's 30 crore investor deck.** A rival claimed 30 to 40 crore Indians would invest within years. Lesson: overselling market size to raise money traps you. "I'm like dude what are they smoking" (S012) (also S017)
11. **The Rs 100 crore salary backlash.** Taking money as salary looked ugly in a poor country; a buyback or dividend would have drawn no fire. Lesson: how you take money out matters as much as how much. (S012)
12. **The regulatory hit of 2024 to 2025.** SEBI's F&O changes cut brokerage revenue about 40 percent year on year; strong net worth and a lean team absorbed it. Lesson: build buffers before the storm. "This risk has now crystallised with the increase in STT on options and the reduction of expiries to two weekly contracts on options." (S002) (also S003)
13. **Lost Rs 50 crore in a day.** A gold and silver crash cost the firm Rs 50 crore in one day through client leverage. Lesson: a leverage business carries tail risk; keep reserves. (S011)
14. **Stroke in 2024.** After his father's death in December 2023, he had a stroke in January 2024 and slowly recovered. Lesson he draws: what doesn't kill you makes you stronger; sleep and health matter. (S002, S003)
15. **Late to video and IPL.** In 2020 to 2021 Zerodha was busy serving the flood of users while funded rivals spent on video and IPL ads and reached new audiences. Lesson: a miss, even for a no-ads company. "that's a miss from our side." (S011)
