# Books: Amitabh Bachchan

Built from 53 sources: 42 of his own daily blog posts (2012 to 2026) and 11 articles about the ABCL collapse and his comeback. No book text was fetched; summaries are in my own words.

He has written no business books. His thinking lives in his daily blog, so the entries below are the blog's recurring series and themes, then the books (mostly his father's) that he says he draws lessons from.

## Recurring series and themes on the blog

### The daily blog itself (the DAY count)
- **What it is:** A numbered, near-daily post to his fans, whom he calls the Ef (extended family), written late at night after work, opening with birthday greetings and closing with "Love". He treats the daily habit as a duty and as his own channel, separate from the press.
- **Core idea for this seat:** own the channel and never break the chain; speak there rather than in sound bites.
- **Sources:** S014 to S055 (all 42 blog sources); the duty is stated in S015, S019, S030, S039.

### The ABCL debt and repayment (told by him in interviews, retold by others)
- **What it is:** Not a series he wrote, but the story the press keeps returning to: ABCL's collapse in the late 1990s with about Rs 90 crore owed, creditors at his door, Prateeksha attached, a walk to Yash Chopra for work, Mohabbatein, KBC, commercials in lieu of interest, and full repayment.
- **Core idea:** repay everyone, in work if not cash, through the skill that earns, and keep the company alive.
- **Sources:** S001, S002, S004, S005, S007, S008, S009.

### Work at any age
- **What it is:** His most repeated theme from about 2016 on: work is the essence of life, idleness is decline, and suggestions to rest are a plot to stop him. In 2014 he wrote that the old must give way to the new (S018); from 2025 he writes that sitting back is a defeat (S052). In October 2026 he is under strict medical rest (S046).
- **Sources:** S011, S013, S018, S026, S037, S044, S046, S048, S052, S053, S055.

### Critics, trolls, and the press
- **What it is:** A running thread on how to take criticism: let it ground you, use it to improve, never let it change the work, do not feed trolls, mostly stay silent, and answer with results. Later posts read how smear campaigns are built.
- **Sources:** S015, S019, S023, S025, S026, S030, S035, S036, S038, S041, S042, S044, S047, S050, S054.

### Replacement, decline, and the gracious exit
- **What it is:** Posts on the day a public name is replaced: public memory is short, false ego is the error, accept the smaller profile, keep working inside it.
- **Sources:** S026, S028, S029, S031, S032, S049, S052, S055.

### KBC: comeback stories and the Karmveer episodes
- **What it is:** He uses Kaun Banega Crorepati, the show that pulled him out of debt, as a source of lessons: the 2020 season theme of coming back from a setback (S037), contestants who keep getting up (S050), the poor contestant who won in minutes what would take 21 years of salary (S043), and Karmveer episodes honouring people who serve. He insists the live audience and the backroom crew make the show.
- **Sources:** S017, S031, S034, S037, S038, S043, S047, S048, S050, S053.

### Quiet giving
- **What it is:** Posts on charity done without PR: farmers' loans paid off with no-dues documents handed over, COVID relief, soldiers' families. He discloses it only when the press has found it or when it might get others to give.
- **Sources:** S022, S031, S039, S041, S046, S047, S048.

### Sundays at the gates
- **What it is:** The weekly crowd of well-wishers outside his house (Prateeksha from 1982, Jalsa from 1993, per S052). He treats it as proof that a name cannot be manufactured and as the source of his energy.
- **Sources:** S019, S027, S044, S050, S052, S055.

## Books he draws lessons from

### Harivansh Rai Bachchan's poetry and prose (his father, Babuji)
- **What it is:** The most cited source of his values. When in doubt he turns to his father's teachings (S047) and asks what his parents would have done (S050). The rule give and forget is his father's (S039).
- **Works named in the corpus:**
  - *Madhushala* (poem collection): he published an illustrated English and Hindi edition so a younger generation meets his father's work (S030).
  - *Buddha aur Naachghar* (poem): he takes from it the idea that desire is the root of pain, and ties it to setting limits on wanting more (S043).
  - *Pravas ki Diary* (his father's Cambridge diary): seeing a thing is not the same as understanding it, and modern ideas are stronger with the weight of tradition behind them (S049).
  - His father's autobiography: taught him family history he had not known (S039).
- **Other lessons from his father in the corpus:** simplicity with strength (S029); keeping the full family name and the identity behind it (S033).
- **Sources:** S029, S030, S033, S036, S039, S043, S047, S049, S050.

### Jan Gita
- **What it is:** A translation of the Gita into the language of Tulsidas's Ramayan. He reads one page each morning, a habit his father left him, and says it strengthens him.
- **Sources:** S024.

### Other books mentioned in passing
- *Sapiens*: relayed from his daughter's reading, on gossip binding groups together (S027).
- Mark Manuel's book on serial killers, for which he wrote the foreword (S028).
- *Three Rivers and a Tree* by Neelum Saran Gour, about Allahabad University, where his father taught (S032).
- None of these shape his decisions in the corpus.
