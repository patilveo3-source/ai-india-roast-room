# Stories: Amitabh Bachchan

Built from the four extraction batches over 53 sources (42 blog posts, 2012 to 2026, and 11 articles on ABCL and the comeback). 19 stories he tells or that are told about him, most-used first, with the lesson. Accounts by others are marked reported by others. Not in the notes, so not here: the radio rejection, the "too tall" rejections, and a COVID hospitalisation; the Coolie injury appears only as a passing mention (S036).

1. **ABCL collapses and the creditors come to the door.** His company, aiming for Rs 1,000 crore, sank under box-office failures and a Miss India sponsorship, leaving about Rs 90 crore of debt, 55 legal cases and his home attached by banks (company figures reported by others, S001, S005). His own memory: "I can never forget how creditors used to land at our door, be abusive, threatening and demanding," (S002, 2013 interview; also S007). Lesson: public failure is humiliation first and money second, and you do not run from it (S001, S002, S007, S008, S017).

2. **Walking to Yash Chopra's house to ask for work.** With no films and no money, he asked himself what he could do, answered that he could act, and walked to the filmmaker who lived behind his house: "I implored him to give me work." (S002) Chopra gave him Mohabbatein (2000). Lesson: go back to the one skill that pays, and ask in person, plainly, with pride set aside (S001, S002, S008).

3. **KBC as the lifeline.** In 2000 he took a television quiz show when no top film star would go to the small screen and some laughed (reported by others, S009). "KBC gave me a second life. It was my lifeline," (S001) Reported by others: Rs 15 crore for 85 episodes helped pay the creditors (S005). He also learned he had tuberculosis just as KBC began (S022). Lesson: the job your peers look down on can be the one that saves you (S001, S005, S009, S022).

4. **Paying Doordarshan's interest in commercials.** He repaid every creditor, and when Doordarshan asked for interest he paid it in work: "I did commercials in lieu for them." (S002; same in S007) He closed by saying he had "repaid my entire debt of Rs 90 crore and am starting afresh," (S008). Lesson: pay in whatever currency the creditor accepts, and finish the whole debt (S002, S007, S008).

5. **Refusing the Ambani money.** Reported by others: Dhirubhai Ambani had his son offer money; Bachchan declined, and years later Dhirubhai praised him for standing up on his own strength, words Bachchan said meant more than money. His own line: "I was deeply moved by his generosity, but I couldn’t bring myself to accept it," (S001). Lesson: a rescue that costs your self-respect costs your name too (S001).

6. **The MBAs who ran ABCL.** Reported by others only (Chakor Doshi, ex-executives): ABCL went from 10 to 120 staff in a year, spread into too many businesses, and put a CEO in too early, while Bachchan gave the team free rein and hesitated to step in. Insiders say he concluded the hired managers did not understand the industry (S004). Paired with his later rule: "There is no substitute to personal attention and care .. none .. long may this survive" (S028). Lesson: delegate, but never stop checking (S004, S025, S028, S045).

7. **Two shifts a day at 58.** Reported by others (producer Suneel Darshan): after the debt he shot 9 to 6 on Kabhi Khushi Kabhie Gham and 7 pm to 2 am on another film, and offered to help fix a broken schedule (S007). Lesson: the comeback is paid for in hours (S007, S023, S037).

8. **The ITC no in Kolkata.** In the early 1960s ITC interviewed him for a job and turned him down; years later an ITC product head came to his set as a fan. "If they could build it .. we could surmount it too .. or should attempt to surmount it too …" (S023). He also worked his first job in a coal company office sent to the mines (S028). Lesson: a rejection is an obstacle people built, so people can climb it (S023, S028, S032).

9. **The two annas and the shoes under the pillow.** As a boy he could not join a school cricket club because the fee could not be found, and owned one new pair of shoes in five years: "the one new pair of shoes which I got once in 5 years was my dearest possession, and that I slept with them under my pillow" (S016). He went on to deliver "a million times that 12.5" (S030). Lesson: a small denial, remembered, becomes lifelong drive and a sense of limits (S016, S030, S032).

10. **"Your sun has set."** When Lawaris released, a journalist wrote that his sun had begun to set. Decades later: "my sun has indeed set, and I am grateful that it took a little longer perhaps than was predicted" (S026). He replays the critic's line, "the Sun has set on your career Mr Bachchan, its time to accept it and rest in peace" (S032), and argues for one more push. Lesson: outlast the obituary, but accept the change (S026, S032).

11. **The Sharabi hand in the pocket.** A Diwali firecracker wrecked his hand before Sharabi. Delay was not an option, so "I would put the burnt bandaged hand in my pocket throughout the film" (S033), and it became a signature style: "painful throughout the film but it worked" (S024). Lesson: turn a constraint into a choice and protect the schedule (S024, S033).

12. **Barefoot on anaesthetic for DON.** With blistered feet on a deadline he "took anaesthesia injections in the feet and worked the 4-5 days that took to shoot it" (S036). No distributor wanted the film, thinking the title was an underwear brand, and it ran for decades. He also kept shooting Te3n with a cracked rib (S023). Lesson: keep the commitment through pain, and early rejection of a product is not the verdict (S023, S036).

13. **Walking out after 53 years.** In May 2022 he left a professional engagement for the first time, "never before in the 53 years - 1969 to 2022 - of reason to do this" (S042), because he felt taken for granted. Lesson: loyalty is long, but once trust breaks it does not repair (S042).

14. **The troll and "Inquilab Srivastava".** A troll mocked his surname and faith on Hindi Day; he explained his father dropped the caste name and answered: "the number of times you shall thrash and beat me to the ground , I shall get up and thrash and beat you back, the same number of times .." (S038). This is his mother's rule in action: "get back there beat them back and come home with a smile .." (S035). Lesson: answer once, factually and hard, then walk on (S035, S038).

15. **The hotel owner and the flower bed.** He tells of a hotel-chain owner who arrives unannounced to check one flower bed, and of Henry Ford: "A certain Mr Ford apparently knew the first names of all his employees working in his factory and manufacturing plant" (S028). He does the same with surprise visits to his own staff (S045). Lesson: the owner audits in person; delegation without checking fails (S028, S045).

16. **Paying off farmers' loans.** Given lists by a Chief Minister's office, he went to his own bank and cleared farmers' loans, 360 in one round, "giving each farmer the document that they did not owe any more" (S039); later over 1,500 in all (S039). Lesson: having been the debtor, he treats clearing debt as the most direct help, done quietly and within his means (S031, S039).

17. **Garfield Sobers and the middle ball.** Asked how he scored a hundred after rum in the dressing room, Sobers said: "i was seeing 3 balls coming at me from the bowlers .. I was hitting the middle one" (S044). He uses it when his advisers argue over his next venture. Lesson: too many options paralyse; commit to one and own the call (S044).

18. **Insisting on the full name.** In 1969 his name was too long for the credits and he was asked to shorten it; "on my insistence the full name started appearing on the credits" (S033). Lesson: the name is the asset; do not trade it away for convenience (S033).

19. **Getting out of bed to redo a finished job.** In December 2025, past 83, he declared a piece of work done, then left his sleep to improve it, asking "was it done to its best .. was it delivered to its best .. shall it be accepted as its best" (S054). Lesson: his own standard moves the finish line; the work is never fully done (S049, S054).
