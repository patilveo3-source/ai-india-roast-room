# Frameworks: Amitabh Bachchan

Built from 53 sources: 42 of his own daily blog posts (2012 to 2026) and 11 articles about the ABCL collapse (late 1990s, about Rs 90 crore of debt), his repayment, his comeback through Mohabbatein and KBC, and his working past 80.

25 frameworks. He names very few, so most are *(unnamed)* patterns read across sources. Order is most-used first, with the debt and comeback frameworks lifted to the top because they answer this seat's question. "Sources" counts distinct sources that use the idea (ids listed). Sources marked "reported" are articles where others describe him, not his own words. The How-to-score lines are scoring guidance for the council, built from his stated rules; they are not quotes.

---

## 1. Repay every rupee, keep the company alive *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** After ABCL failed with about Rs 90 crore owed, he refused to let the company die or to walk away from any creditor. Returning other people's money was an ethical duty, and where cash ran short he paid in work.
- **Parts:** Keep the entity alive; list everyone owed, down to the smallest; repay in full, including interest; when the creditor will take it, pay in kind (he did commercials for Doordarshan in lieu of interest); earn the money back through the one skill that pays; then start afresh small (ABCL became AB Corp and returned to production with one film, reported in S005).
- **When to use:** A founder facing default, a failed launch that leaves vendors, investors, or customers out of pocket, or a plan to shut down and leave debts behind.
- **How to score:** 5 if there is a named plan to repay every creditor in full, with payment in kind where cash is short, and the founder will carry the work personally. 3 if repayment depends on one lucky win. 1 if the plan is to let the company die, settle for less, or leave creditors unpaid while the founder moves on.
- **Quote:** "I was operating on the principle that the Corporation (ABCL, or Amitabh Bachchan Corporation Ltd) must not die, I had to ensure that I paid back every sow that I owed to a vast army of people." (S008) "When they asked for the interest component, I did commercials in lieu for them." (S002)
- **Sources:** 5 (S001, S002, S005 reported, S007, S008). Echoed later in how he clears other people's debts: he paid off farmers' bank loans and handed each a no-dues document (S031, S039).

## 2. What can you do? Go back to it *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** At the bottom, he sat down, weighed his options, and found one answer: the skill that had always earned. Then he swallowed his pride, walked to a neighbour who could give work (Yash Chopra), and said plainly that he was bankrupt.
- **Parts:** Sit and evaluate the scenarios; name the one thing you know how to do; ask in person, plainly, someone who can give you work; take every job, including ones peers look down on (television, commercials); work double shifts until the debt is gone (two shifts a day at 58, reported in S007).
- **When to use:** After a public failure, when a founder is tempted to launch a new, unrelated venture to escape the shame of the old one.
- **How to score:** 5 if the comeback rests on the founder's proven earning skill and a named person to ask. 3 if the skill is real but the founder will not ask anyone. 1 if the comeback is a fresh bet in an unfamiliar field, or waits for pride to heal first.
- **Quote:** "I sat one day and asked myself, ‘What can you do?’ The answer was simple, ‘You are an actor, go back to acting,’" (S001) "went directly to Yash Chopraji and told him that I was bankrupt. I had no films." (S008)
- **Sources:** 5 (S001, S002, S007 reported, S008, S009 reported).

## 3. Stand and face it, do not run *(unnamed; the troubled-times arc)*
- **What it is:** His account of the bad years: dread, sleepless nights, staring at the problem, even astrologers and stones, and none of it helped. What helped was turning to face the thing, fighting it, and getting up each time he was knocked down.
- **Parts:** Rest first, because a rested mind becomes a practical one (S017); stop staring at the problem; stand square and face it; fight rather than drift, even if you lose; get up, dust off, return to the fight (his mother's rule: do not bring tears home, go back and come home with a smile, S035); learn the lesson so the error is not repeated.
- **When to use:** A founder hiding from creditors, investors, the press, or a bad number; any plan whose core move is delay or avoidance.
- **How to score:** 5 if the founder is going toward the problem in person this week. 3 if they will face it once some condition is met. 1 if the plan is silence toward the people owed, a quiet exit, or waiting for it to blow over.
- **Quote:** "But nothing comes … for nothing ever came from staring at your problem and your condition." (S017) "the more you run away the greater the speed with which it shall catch up with you" (S032) "get thrown down .. but get up , brush off the dirt and muck and .. stand up, and face the music" (S038)
- **Sources:** 8 (S017, S019, S020, S032, S035, S038, S047, S052).

## 4. Fight or accept the day you are replaced
- **What it is:** Every name in public life is eventually replaced. You can fight for the old place and be sidestepped, or accept a smaller profile. He says most people fight, and refusing the change is the gravest error. His view on what comes after has shifted.
- **Parts:** Know it will happen to you; do the honest self-audit alone, in front of the mirror; drop the false ego about the old position; accept the altered profile; keep working inside it.
- **How his view changed:** 2014: "It is change that is needed. The old must give way to the fresh and the new." (S018) 2018: "step back .. gracefully .. retire to the common room .. put up the feet" (S029) 2024: the creating phase has passed, so flow with the industry rather than make waves (S049). **Newer, 2025:** "NO .. sitting back is a defeat of life - you are giving up" (S052). His working reconciliation, inferred from S029, S049, S052: accept the smaller role and lesser light, but never stop working.
- **When to use:** A founder whose category, product, or personal brand has been overtaken; a founder deciding between clinging to the old position and taking a smaller role.
- **How to score:** 5 if the founder accepts the new, smaller position and keeps working at full effort. 3 if they accept it but plan to coast. 1 if the plan depends on reclaiming the old place by force or by denial.
- **Quote:** "It happens to the greatest .. and it shall happen to you too .. it may take longer but it shall .." (S028)
- **Sources:** 8 (S018, S026, S028, S029, S031, S032, S049, S052).

## 5. Silence as a wall; keep the door shut *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Against a hostile critic or a story that will go badly, there are two answers: remove the cause by changing, or grow thick skin and say nothing. Silence neither accepts nor rejects, and the attacker wastes the energy.
- **Parts:** Ask whether the criticism points at something real; if so, change it; if not, stay silent; weigh pros and cons before saying anything in public, because words get lifted and twisted (S035); when much has happened in a day, keep quiet (S031); step back from an absurd argument rather than join it (S044).
- **How his view changed:** 2014 to 2022 he holds mostly to silence (S019, S026, S038). **Newer, 2023:** sometimes a courteous reply in the same language works: "but at times it is important to answer , to respond with the same language the courteous repartie occured .. and the results are often quite in favour" (S047).
- **When to use:** A founder under public attack, deciding whether to post a rebuttal, hold a press call, or argue online.
- **How to score:** 5 if the founder either fixes the real cause or stays silent and works. 3 if one short, courteous factual reply is planned, then silence. 1 if the plan is a public fight, a thread of rebuttals, or naming the attackers.
- **Quote:** "The solution is two fold. Get rid of the issue, by change. Or, the other, get a hard skinned clothing that does not allow penetration into the body .." (S019) "it is prudent most times to keep the trap shut" (S026)
- **Sources:** 8 (S019, S026, S031, S035, S038, S044, S047, S054).

## 6. Delegate, then audit in person *(unnamed; personal-touch management)*
- **What it is:** Delegation fails unless the owner checks personally, by surprise, on the ground. He admits he is poor at choosing people, so his system is to check rather than to trust the hire.
- **Parts:** Delegate the work; make surprise visits; check, ask, and monitor salaried staff yourself; take over what you cannot trust another to do to your taste (S029). The time cost is small and the effect large (S045).
- **Context:** Reported by others in S004: at ABCL he hired a corporate management team, staff grew from 10 to 120 in a year, and he gave the team free rein and was hesitant to interfere. His later insistence on personal monitoring reads as the lesson of that failure (inferred from S004, S028, S045).
- **When to use:** A founder about to hand a failing area to a new executive, or a founder who has not looked at the work of a delegated team in months.
- **How to score:** 5 if the founder will personally inspect the delegated work on a set rhythm, including unannounced checks. 3 if they rely on reports. 1 if a hired team runs a high-stakes area with no owner checking it.
- **Quote:** "Efficiency of delegation, shall disrupt and be mismanaged, if monitoring of a personal nature does not take place" (S028) "get down on your own hands and knees and check, ask, monitor all that needs to be done, by so called professional salaried staff" (S045)
- **Limits:** "my HR is bad .. really bad .. I can never be smart enough to chose the right people to do the right work for me .. so I end up doing it myself" (S025)
- **Sources:** 8 (S004 reported, S025, S028, S029, S036, S045, S051, S052).

## 7. Answer with the work *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** He does not defend his choices with words. The reply to critics, doubters, and people who ask why he still works at 81 is the result itself.
- **Parts:** Refuse to explain at length; keep working; let the result reply; stay quiet in victory. He says in Hindi that there is no bigger answer than work, and that every reply only gives the questioner more work (S053, paraphrased).
- **When to use:** A founder who wants to answer a public doubter, a hostile review, or a down round with a statement instead of a shipped result.
- **How to score:** 5 if the plan answers the doubt with a measurable result on a date. 1 if the plan is mostly messaging.
- **Quote:** "I work. Period. Got a problem with that? Well then get to work and find out." (S011) "nothing gives apt response than the result" (S041)
- **Sources:** 6 (S011, S013, S030, S039, S041, S053).

## 8. Abuse as fuel *(unnamed; criticism as fire coals)*
- **What it is:** Criticism on a public platform keeps you grounded and shows you how others see you. He claims to welcome abuse because it pushes him to improve, while refusing it the attention it wants.
- **Parts:** Feel the anger, but never let it change the work (S023); remember that those who receive the work far outnumber those who object; take what is true and improve; walk over the criticism like hot coals to the cool ground beyond (S050).
- **When to use:** After a bad launch, harsh reviews, or a public pile-on.
- **How to score:** 5 if the founder can name what the criticism got right and what will change. 3 if they shrug it off whole. 1 if they change the core product to quiet a loud minority, or answer by fighting.
- **Quote:** "I do not have time to attend to abuse .. I love it .. actually .. for it provokes me to betterment" (S030) "Abuse and criticism is healthy on such platforms. They keep you grounded, and teach you where and what others may think of you, even if you live in an exalted haven of purity and correctness" (S025)
- **Sources:** 6 (S023, S025, S030, S036, S042, S050).

## 9. The routine of perfection; job done, then reflect *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Repeat the work until you are personally satisfied, then question the finished job anyway and ask for another take. The effort compounds even when the result falls short.
- **Parts:** Do it again and again; at the finish ask three questions: was it done to its best, delivered to its best, will it be accepted as its best (S054); assume nothing is ever perfect (S037); go back and improve, even after calling it done.
- **When to use:** Deciding whether a product, pitch, or relaunch is ready to face the public a second time.
- **How to score:** 5 if the founder can answer all three questions with evidence. 3 if two of three. 1 if the relaunch repeats the old work unchanged.
- **Quote:** "I do and do again and again .. that is the routine of perfection .. I may not get it right even then .. but there is the bank of effort and desire to excel" (S020) "was it done to its best .. was it delivered to its best .. shall it be accepted as its best" (S054)
- **Sources:** 6 (S016, S020, S037, S044, S049, S054).

## 10. COMEBACK from a SETBACK *(his KBC 2020 season theme)*
- **What it is:** His own label, written on the blog in capitals as "COMBACK from a SETBACK" (S037): the person in the hot seat is someone returning from a loss, and what matters is sincerity, resolve, and continuity. The roots decide whether you grow back.
- **Parts:** Check that the roots (skill, name, relationships) are secure even if the leaves (money, status) have fallen; bring continuity; look for the solution that pressure forces (S036); accept that what comes back may be less than before (S043).
- **When to use:** Judging whether a fallen company or founder can rebuild, and on what.
- **How to score:** 5 if the core skill, reputation for honesty, and key relationships survived the fall. 3 if one of the three is damaged. 1 if the roots themselves (trust, skill) are gone.
- **Quote:** "the leaves may fall off .. but if the roots are secure it shall grow again" (S038) "perhaps not of the same value as before the issue, but I got it back" (S043)
- **Sources:** 4 (S036, S037, S038, S043).

## 11. Admit it and end the debate *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Admitting a mistake shows character and ends the argument, which frees time for work you trust. Defending a mistake keeps the debate alive.
- **Parts:** Admit quickly; tell the other side they may be right; stop the argument; move the saved time to what you know; consider that you may be the fault (S044).
- **When to use:** After a public error, when the founder is choosing between an apology and a defence.
- **How to score:** 5 if the founder admits the specific error plainly and moves on. 1 if the plan is to argue the facts in public for weeks.
- **Quote:** "Admitting a mistake is not wrong .. it shows character .. once you have admitted it, the burden of its non function is better understood, and all debate on it ends" (S012)
- **Sources:** 4 (S012, S018, S019, S044).

## 12. Bend or break *(the fruit-laden tree)*
- **What it is:** Prominence draws envy and hostility. The branch heavy with fruit should bend in humility; the stiff branch is the one that breaks.
- **Parts:** Expect hostility to rise with success; bend rather than stiffen; keep gains quiet; no brag about beating age or rivals (S034); be silent in the good.
- **When to use:** A founder at a peak, or one whose arrogance is part of why the failure is being enjoyed in public.
- **How to score:** 5 if the founder's public stance is humble and quiet in success. 1 if the plan leans on bravado.
- **Quote:** "Indulge in the opposing or the opposite, and stiffen that branch of adage consumed tree, resist all demands of accepted conduct, and remain stiff and un bendable .. until the breakage of the branch !!!" (S024) "be silent in the good .. be in the quiet of dignity and reserve .. class shall pay" (S041)
- **Sources:** 5 (S024, S032, S034, S041, S048).

## 13. Limits on getting more *(unnamed; the greed trap)*
- **What it is:** Success makes you believe you are indestructible, and having x makes you want 10x. Only you can set your own limit. Later posts turn this into contentment with what you have.
- **Parts:** Notice the jump from x to 10x; admit the temptation; set the limit yourself; treat money-chasing as a weak foundation (S037); take satisfaction as a measure (S050, S052).
- **When to use:** Expansion after a win, especially the kind of overreach that sank ABCL (reported in S004 and S005: a Rs 1,000 crore target and spread into too many businesses).
- **How to score:** 5 if growth stays inside what the founder can fund and oversee. 1 if the plan multiplies scope because the last bet worked.
- **Quote:** "Greed creeps in. The thinking that since I have ‘x’, I must have ‘10x’ overpowers them." (S016) "In the heat of hard earned success, there comes a moment when it is wrongly believed that with it comes indestructibility." (S016)
- **Sources:** 5 (S016, S037, S043, S050, S052).

## 14. Give and forget *(his father's rule)*
- **What it is:** Give without waiting to receive, do it quietly, give from your excess and within your means. He makes it public only when it might get others to copy it.
- **Parts:** Give to give; no PR; do not count the money; tell it only if it will move others to give.
- **When to use:** Mostly a values check, but it shapes how he thinks a name is rebuilt: by deeds done quietly, not by announcing them.
- **How to score:** Guidance: a rebuild plan that relies on publicised good deeds scores lower than one that does them quietly.
- **Quote:** "give and forget .. do not give and wait for receive .. know that it shall never come back .. if it does , good ; if it does not , even better" (S039)
- **Sources:** 7 (S022, S031, S039, S041, S046, S047, S048).

## 15. Own your platform *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Speak through a channel you own and control, daily, instead of through journalists' sound bites or another company's platform. The daily habit keeps the connection unbroken.
- **Parts:** Refuse forced sound bites (S015); write on your own platform; post every day so the chain does not break (S019); keep your own information desk (S042); value the one reader over the numbers (S039).
- **When to use:** Deciding how a founder will tell their side of a public failure.
- **How to score:** 5 if the founder has a direct channel to customers and will use it plainly. 1 if the story depends wholly on press coverage.
- **Quote:** "through a platform, that I shall own and be in control of .. to run and push out in the manner I want, without having to depend on the exigencies of a Twitter" (S030)
- **Sources:** 5 (S015, S019, S030, S039, S042).

## 16. The audience as judge and creditor *(the non performing asset)*
- **What it is:** The public is the final judge and the source of his name. He calls their affection a debt he never wants to repay. Their verdict is fast both ways.
- **Parts:** Let the audience decide; accept likes and dislikes as a daily exam; remember they raise you and drop you equally fast; give them more than their due (S047).
- **When to use:** Judging whether customers, not insiders, will forgive a failure.
- **How to score:** 5 if real customers have shown they will come back. 1 if only insiders and investors are on side.
- **Quote:** "I am in debt for this .. and may this debt remain ever and never be repaid by me .. for I would wish to be ever in the modality of a non performing asset" (S027) "for likes and dislikes change rapidly .. as rapidly as they make you rise , equally rapidly they bring you down" (S050)
- **Sources:** 5 (S021, S027, S028, S043, S050).

## 17. Who judges the work *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Whose verdict counts on the quality of your work. His answer has shifted.
- **How his view changed:** 2015: let others praise you; if they do not, treat it as your own failing and improve. "What good shall it reflect if the self were to appraise or praise the self." (S021) 2017: awards belong to others, so celebrate inside. "the decision taken for reward is not your own, it belongs to the other .. the other shall decide for posterity, value worth and recognition" (S026) 2018: opinions of you serve the opinion holder's needs (S030). **Newer, 2025 to 2026:** your own assessment comes first. "your own silent assessment may be advised for personal perfection" (S054)
- **When to use:** A founder deciding whether a failure was bad work or bad reception.
- **How to score:** Guidance: trust the founder's own hard assessment of the work, checked against what customers do, over press or award verdicts.
- **Sources:** 5 (S021, S026, S030, S051, S054).

## 18. Flow with the tide or make your own waves
- **What it is:** When the industry changes, adapt or opt out. At his age he chooses to flow and to care about taking part more than about the result.
- **Parts:** Accept change as inevitable; learn the new technology or consciously stay in your own lane (S045); do not argue with the new generation about their medium (S042).
- **When to use:** An older company or founder facing a platform shift.
- **How to score:** 5 if the founder picks one path deliberately. 1 if they half-adapt and resent it.
- **Quote:** "Flow with the tide .. or create your own waves for the tides to follow" (S049)
- **Sources:** 5 (S018, S036, S042, S045, S049).

## 19. Own your decision; hit the middle ball *(the Garfield Sobers story)*
- **What it is:** Take advice with caution and decide yourself, so credit and blame are yours. When options blur, pick the middle one and commit.
- **Parts:** Hear the knowers; distrust the know-all; decide; own the result; when too many options breed more options, commit to one.
- **When to use:** A founder stalled by advisers who disagree.
- **How to score:** 5 if one person owns the call. 1 if the decision is spread across advisers so that no one owns the failure.
- **Quote:** "if it works your decide .. if not your decide .. none from the other side of the wall to take credit or snipe at the failure" (S044) "i was seeing 3 balls coming at me from the bowlers .. I was hitting the middle one" (S044)
- **Sources:** 4 (S012, S041, S044, S045).

## 20. Constraint as style *(the Sharabi hand)*
- **What it is:** When an injury or problem would wreck the schedule, find a workaround that looks like a choice and keep the commitment. His burnt hand, hidden in his pocket for a whole film, became a signature.
- **Parts:** Refuse the delay; find the workaround; keep working through pain on a deadline (S023, S036).
- **When to use:** A setback mid-delivery, where a founder is tempted to delay a promised date.
- **How to score:** 5 if the plan keeps the promise with a visible workaround. 1 if it delays without one.
- **Quote:** "In order to not rock the boat, I thought of an idea .. I would put the burnt bandaged hand in my pocket throughout the film" (S033)
- **Sources:** 4 (S023, S024, S033, S036).

## 21. The provider's duty *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Fear for the family's security is what keeps him working, and a man should leave the world having provided so those left behind do not suffer insult or penury.
- **When to use:** Weighing a public-failure risk that would fall on dependents, or a founder deciding how much personal guarantee to sign.
- **How to score:** Guidance: a bet that could leave dependents in penury scores low whatever its upside.
- **Quote:** "The fear of security, of responsibility towards the state of the family and dependents, I admit, does keep you going." (S016) "you want to leave the World by the provide for those that remained dear .. they should not suffer, penury, defeat insult" (S048)
- **Sources:** 3 (S016, S048, S050).

## 22. Anatomy of a smear *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Attacks on a famous name are indirect: go after the associates first, spread assumption as fact, and leave the target to pay to correct it. A sudden storm of comments signals falling numbers, a coming launch, or a rival aiming at the top (S038).
- **When to use:** Reading a reputational attack before deciding whether to answer it.
- **How to score:** Guidance: if the attack is assumption spread as fact, do not spend to correct it; protect the associates.
- **Quote:** "to demolish the King first demolish his parrot" (S035) "assume and popularise as fact .. let those that wish to correct it , spend time energy and commercials to rectify it" (S054)
- **Sources:** 4 (S035, S038, S050, S054).

## 23. Half first, then the hundred
- **What it is:** Do not aim for 100 at once. Settle for half, reach it, make it the base, then go for the rest. Go slowly if you must, but do not stop.
- **When to use:** Setting comeback targets after a fall.
- **How to score:** 5 if the first target is modest, dated, and reachable. 1 if the plan aims to regain everything at once.
- **Quote:** "Expect not the 100 ; settle for its half, attain it and making that the base strive for the 100 .. after all it shall be just a 50 more" (S030) "It does not matter how slowly you go so long as you do not stop" (S032)
- **Sources:** 3 (S030, S032, S051).

## 24. Survive first; the serious issue first *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** When a serious problem arrives, routine steps back and the immediate threat is handled first. Plan for the worst. Postponing to protect people is maturity, not surrender.
- **When to use:** Triage in a crisis with many demands at once.
- **How to score:** 5 if the plan names the one threat to survival and handles it first. 1 if it spreads effort evenly.
- **Quote:** "the only way to survive a hundred and one jobs and chores to do is to .. survive first .. then attend to the jobs" (S046) "plan the worst and hope for the best" (S036)
- **Sources:** 3 (S036, S041, S046).

## 25. The three-day window
- **What it is:** A film's fate is now decided on its first Friday, Saturday, and Sunday, so the whole promotion effort has to land on those days, despite the cost.
- **When to use:** Any launch (or relaunch) where first-weekend reception sets the public story.
- **How to score:** 5 if the launch effort is concentrated on the first days. 1 if spending is spread thin after the verdict is already in.
- **Quote:** "the life of a film has now been limited to those 3 vital days - the Friday Saturday and the Sunday .. all that needs to happen then has to happen then" (S025)
- **Sources:** 3 (S023, S025, S043).
