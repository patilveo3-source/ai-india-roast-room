# Positions: Amitabh Bachchan

Built from the four extraction batches over 53 sources: 42 of his own daily blog posts (2012 to 2026) and 11 articles on the ABCL bankruptcy, the repayment, and working past 80. 33 topics, most-used first (the last groups the topics with no evidence). Each line carries a source id and a date; article quotes carry the interview date where the notes give one (S002 is a 2013 interview, S006 a 2000 interview). Where his view moved, the versions sit side by side and the newer one is marked. Lines marked "reported by others" are not his words.

## Criticism, trolls, and abuse
- **Stance:** Feel it, never let it change the work, do not feed it, and answer with work and results rather than replies. He swings between welcoming abuse as fuel and refusing to engage at all. 14 sources: S019, S022, S023, S025, S028, S029, S030, S036, S038, S039, S047, S050, S053, S054.
- **Over time:**
  - 2014-05: two choices, change or thick skin: "The solution is two fold. Get rid of the issue, by change. Or, the other, get a hard skinned clothing that does not allow penetration into the body .." (S019) and "Silence is not an indicator of acceptance, or rejection" (S019)
  - 2016-01: "It angers .. it builds resentment .. BUT .. we must NOT and NEVER allow that to affect what we do" (S023) The receivers outnumber the objectors (S023).
  - 2016-09: "Abuse and criticism is healthy on such platforms. They keep you grounded" (S025)
  - 2018-05: do not give the troll what he wants: "attention given to such translates into what the troller actually wanted - NOTICE" (S030) yet "I do not have time to attend to abuse .. I love it .. actually .. for it provokes me to betterment" (S030) People judge to suit themselves: "it was ALWAYS them not me" (S030)
  - 2021-01: "beat then to hold ones peace and walk on" (S038)
  - 2023-09: sometimes answer: "but at times it is important to answer , to respond with the same language the courteous repartie occured .. and the results are often quite in favour" (S047)
  - 2024-09: "each criticism is the bed of steaming fire coals that compel you to walk over them" (S050)
  - 2025-09 (newest): work is the only reply; replying only multiplies the questions (S053, Hindi line, paraphrased).
- **Read:** consistent core (do not let it touch the work), inconsistent tactics. Inferred from S030, S038, S047, S053: the later posts lean toward silence plus results over open engagement.

## Debt and the ABCL collapse
- **Stance:** Debt is the darkest thing that can happen to a working person, and the shame is worse than the money. He never treats default as an option. 6 sources: S001, S002, S007, S008, S031, S027.
- 2013 interview: "Without a doubt that was one of the darkest moments in my 44-year professional career." (S002)
- 2021 article: "There was a sword hanging on my head all the time. I spent many sleepless nights." (S008)
- 2025 article: "Everything I owned was gone, my bank balance was zero, my company had failed, and there were creditors outside every day. It was humiliating," (S001)
- He later pays off other people's debts: 360 farmer loans, "within whatever means of my personal" (S031, 2018-09). He calls the fans' love the one debt he wants never to repay: "I am in debt for this .. and may this debt remain ever and never be repaid by me" (S027, 2017-05)

## Paying creditors
- **Stance:** Repay every rupee, as an ethical duty, and pay in work where you cannot pay in cash. Keep the company alive while you do it. 4 sources: S002, S007, S008, S005 (reported).
- "I felt it wasn't ethically right not to return the money," (S008, 2021 article)
- "I paid back one and all, including Doordarshan. When they asked for the interest component, I did commercials in lieu for them." (S002, 2013 interview; same line in S007)
- "I was operating on the principle that the Corporation (ABCL, or Amitabh Bachchan Corporation Ltd) must not die, I had to ensure that I paid back every sow that I owed to a vast army of people." (S008)
- Reported by others: KBC fees and endorsements paid the creditors, and ABCL was renamed AB Corp in 2001 (S005, 2010).

## Failure in public and the comeback
- **Stance:** One public failure spreads to everything and people stop believing; the answer is to stand and face it, not run, then get up and go again. 9 sources: S001, S008, S017, S020, S024, S032, S035, S037, S038.
- "When one aspect of your life goes wrong, everything else follows. People stop believing in you," (S001, 2025 article)
- 2013-09: "I am tired of running … I’d rather stand square and face it now." (S017) "But if I drop I shall have dropped, not run .." (S017)
- 2014-09: defeat becomes fuel: "in its defiance come the strength to succeed, to beat that which had beaten you, betrayed you, endangered you, had put hurdles before you .." (S020)
- 2020-09: his framing for KBC season 12: "COMBACK from a SETBACK" (S037)
- 2021-01: "get thrown down .. but get up , brush off the dirt and muck and .. stand up, and face the music" (S038)
- Peaks and troughs are normal: "We all go through these phases; peaks and troughs are what life is all about." (S008)

## Rebuilding after ruin: go back to the one skill that pays
- **Stance:** List what you can actually do, pick the skill that earns, swallow pride, and ask someone in person for work. 3 sources: S001, S002, S008.
- "I sat one day and asked myself, ‘What can you do?’ The answer was simple, ‘You are an actor, go back to acting,’" (S001)
- "I looked at the options before me and evaluated different scenarios. The answer came pat - I know how to act." (S002, 2013 interview)
- "went directly to Yash Chopraji and told him that I was bankrupt. I had no films." (S008)

## Bailouts and outside help
- **Stance:** Accept moral support, refuse a rescue you cannot square with your pride. 3 sources: S001, S008, S018.
- On an offer of money from the Ambanis (reported in S001): "I was deeply moved by his generosity, but I couldn’t bring myself to accept it," (S001)
- "They joined hands not to put money, but they gave me moral support and strength," (S008)
- He prizes quiet help most: "to me that seems the most graceful and subtle way of expressing your generosity and dignity in the matter .." (S018, 2014-01)

## Partners, managers, and delegation
- **Stance:** Delegate, but audit personally and by surprise; he admits he is bad at picking people. 7 sources: S025, S028, S029, S036, S045, S051, S052, plus S004 (reported).
- **Over time:**
  - Reported by others: at ABCL he gave the hired corporate team free rein and was hesitant to interfere when things went wrong (S004, 2009 article). Not his words.
  - 2016-09: "my HR is bad .. really bad .. I can never be smart enough to chose the right people to do the right work for me .. so I end up doing it myself" (S025)
  - 2017-09: "Efficiency of delegation, shall disrupt and be mismanaged, if monitoring of a personal nature does not take place" (S028)
  - 2020-05: "the working is of the temperament of the delegate, not yours" (S036)
  - 2023-05: surprise checks keep paid staff honest: "they are sincere and dedicated .. but have the sense to slip away from their duty if not questioned or given surprise visits within their domain" (S045)
  - 2025-07 (newest): "blessed are they that can manage .. they are a league by themselves .. and one that I should have learnt earlier" (S052)
- **Read:** inferred from S004, S025, S028, S045: the ABCL lesson he acts on is never hand over the company and walk away. He contradicts himself on doing it all himself (S025) versus staff doing the checking (S045).
