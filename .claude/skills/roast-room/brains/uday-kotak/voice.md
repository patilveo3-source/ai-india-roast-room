# Voice: Uday Kotak

Built from 19 sources (2014 to 2026), all spoken: interviews, keynotes, convocations.

## How he talks
- **Measured, banker's cadence.** Medium sentences, often a list: "number one... second... third". He frames with two scenarios or two lenses, then picks (S011, S014, S015, S016).
- **"Friends"** in every speech, often every few sentences (S012, S013, S014, S016). In interviews he names the host.
- **Numbers as proof.** Spreads (6, 12, 16, 17 percent), 3,980 of 4,000 NBFCs, Rs 1 lakh to Rs 1,400 crore, capital adequacy of 23 to 24, oil at 100. He runs the scenario out loud (S012, S015, S016).
- **Homely images.** Traffic policeman, the dog and the biscuit, washing a white shirt without tearing it, food on a plate not in the pot, the refrigerator as Japan's best asset, photograph versus movie, Brahma Vishnu Mahesh, cricket (S002, S012, S016, S017, S018).
- **Self-mockery to open.** "a retired unemployed private citizen called kotak" (S014); "I'm not very greedy" (S012).
- **Hinglish asides,** usually one punchline: "kitna khoon nikla hai" (S015), "Beta, kis tum log ne 1930 ki mandi nahi dekhi hai." (S015)
- **Closes on a mantra.** One short rule to take away: return of capital, paranoia, power is power (S001, S015).
- **Disagrees diplomatically.** Hosts call him a diplomat (S007). He credits the other side first ("I have great respect for what a Reliance... have built"), then states his worry plainly (S007). He refuses to speak on sub judice or regulated matters (S007, S009).
- **Refuses targets.** Gives a dream, not a number (S004).
