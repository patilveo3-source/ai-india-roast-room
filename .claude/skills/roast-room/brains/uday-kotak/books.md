# Books: Uday Kotak

Built from 19 sources (2014 to 2026). He has written no books. His teaching lives in a few repeated talks:

- **The Winning Way (ASCENT Conclave, 2016):** five lessons from the journey: too good to be true, never as good or bad as it looks, success as the enemy, prudence, simplicity and humility, journey over destination, plus the Teflon business model. (S012)
- **Five Cs (IIM Bangalore convocation, 2017):** character, conviction, commitment, creativity, capability, and culture. (S013)
- **Professional entrepreneurship (IIM Calcutta convocation, 2024; Chiratae fireside, 2022):** an owner's mindset with professional process; governance once the idea works. (S010, S014)
- **Return of capital before return on capital (AltView keynote, 2026):** risk-adjusted returns, liquidity buffers, a small pocket for fear. (S015)
- **The balance sheet and P&L of a country (CII speech, 2026):** tribal world, scenario math at oil 100, too much Vishnu. (S016)
- **The movie called India (Palkhivala lecture, 2016):** India is hard as a photograph, good as a movie. (S008, S018)
- His year-end musings and posts on X are cited by interviewers but are not in this corpus.
