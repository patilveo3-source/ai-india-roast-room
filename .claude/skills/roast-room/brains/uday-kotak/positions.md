# Positions: Uday Kotak

Built from 19 sources (2014 to 2026): long interviews, keynotes, convocation speeches, and one news report. Stances on founder questions, with source ids and dates. Dates for S009 to S018 are inferred from content. Where there is no evidence the topic says "no recorded position". Most-evidenced first, with balance-sheet topics at the top.

## Debt and leverage
- **Stance:** Excess leverage is the thing that kills. Deleverage while capital is available; equity access decides who survives a crisis. 4 sources.
- 2016-11: "therefore no excess leverage you must be able to withstand shocks" (S012)
- 2020-06: "if you have excessive leverage that is your debt to equity ratio is high please go out there the capital markets are in good shape please raise capital and D leverage your businesses" (S002)
- 2021-09: "if you have access to capital particularly equity capital you have a much greater chance of surviving" (S003)
- 2023-10: banks lever 10 to 12 times equity, so they need oversight: "Banks can leverage 10 12 times its Equity so there is a need for proper oversight" (S007)
- 2019-02: short-term funding of illiquid assets is an accident waiting to happen: "lending our investment in very illiquid situation with short term funding" (S009)

## Cash, burn, and runway
- **Stance:** Cash flow over profit. A funded company may burn, but needs a dated path to positive cash. Keep a liquidity buffer. 4 sources.
- 2014-01: "if you burn more cash you're more valuable but I I can't figure that out" (S005)
- 2025-08: "you must have a game plan to see over time what is the cash generation happening in your business" (S001)
- 2026-03: "Keep enough buffer so that you don't have sleepless nights ever with reference to your short-term money." (S015) Do not lock money needed in the next 12 to 36 months into illiquid bets (S015).

## Growth versus caution
- **Stance:** Both, and he admits he was too cautious once. Go for growth, aim big, but cut exposure early when trouble shows.
- 2014-01 (regret): "in retrospect if I had the opportunity I would have scaled up between 2003 and 2008 even faster" (S005)
- 2025-08: "A zero accident policy is a high risk to growth." (S001)
- 1997 (told in 2022-11): cut the book in half by choice (S010).
- 2025-11: "we were never the biggest but we were always that firm which saw opportunity and moved quickly and got it to reasonable size." (S004)

## Raising money: Indian VCs, foreign capital, bootstrapping
- **Stance:** Raise early from anyone willing to take risk; India needs a domestic risk capital base so it does not depend on foreign money that can leave. He bootstrapped on Rs 30 lakh from friends, family, and Anand Mahindra.
- 2022-11: "if there was any even a remote acquaintance who had the guts to put risk Capital you tapped him" (S010)
- 2020-06: "number one priority for India is to develop risk capital base of Indians in India" (S002)
- 2026-05: "I am a strong advocate of building up private equity uh alternate assets and the venture capital industry domestic." (S016) $14 billion of foreign portfolio money left in one month (S016).
- 2014-01: angel and venture funds should count as priority sector lending (S005).

## Valuations, IPOs, and listing on Indian exchanges
- **Stance:** Most hot listings will not survive; price can be right or wrong but the chance of zero is real. In 2025 he judged IPO pricing broadly fair because quality institutions set the price.
- 2021-09: "if i were a betting man i would say a very small percentage of them will be around five to seven years from now" (S003)
- 2021-09: "you should be ready to take a view that 100 rupees can become zero as well" (S003)
- 2025-11: no debacles like three years earlier; investors push back on price (S004, paraphrase).
- 2025-11 (on Kotak's own subsidiaries): no rush to list while embedded value compounds (S004, said by CEO Vaswani; Kotak agreed in substance). Inferred.

## Equity, partners, and control
- **Stance:** Give minority stakes to strong partners but keep control; joint ventures need a goal and an exit.
- 2025-08: "Which is why they were 25% and we were 75." (S001)
- 2016-11: "we could not find out any mathematical way by which two of us could own 51% each" (S012)
- 2022-11: "joint ventures are not forever very difficult joint ventures forever" (S010)

## Acquisitions
- **Stance:** Price as a cash cheque; be patient (ING Vysya watched from 2008, closed 2014); no stupid deals.
- 2022-11: "always think about an acquisition as if you're going to write a check" (S010)
- 2018-02: "we will not rush in to do stupid deals" (S008)

## Pricing and discounting
- **Stance:** Win on a narrow spread with strong risk; never take a premium that exploits scarcity; profit is fine, profiteering is not. No recorded position on rupee discounting or price wars as such.
- 2016-11: cut an 11 percent bank spread to 4 percent: "so I'm not very greedy so I thought this is a decent spread to operate in" (S012)
- 2022-11: refused to charge a premium on scarce cars (S010).
- 2020-06: "I don't think prop making much profits is the wrong thing but profiteering and taking advantage of people is something we need to take care" (S002)

## Costs and overhead
- 2014-01: "for anything which is a wasteful overhead and which is not adding to your business or your Top Line don't waste money but anything which is critical for your business don't save money" (S005)
- 2020-06: in a crisis keep fixed costs low relative to breakeven (S002).

## Government, regulation, and policy risk
- **Stance:** Use policy tailwinds but never depend on them; stay inside the law; respect the regulator in public while asking for minimize-accidents, not zero-accident rules; against retrospective rules.
- 2016-11: "be ready for death in any of your businesses any of your segments and don't depend on government policies to necessarily sustain you" (S012) 85 percent of his business was banned overnight in 1992 (S012).
- 2023-10: "I am regulated and I respect regulation" (S007)
- 2020-06: against retrospective law; single window approvals (S002).
- 2020-10: "let's not be excessively interventionist if the marketplace is developing well" (S011)

## GST and compliance
- No recorded position on GST compliance for founders. 2019-02: called GST, demonetisation, and IBC path-breaking changes that took energy out of the system for years (S009). Treat compliance as part of professionalism and the law (S003).

## Governance
- **Stance:** Trusteeship over raja-praja; one outside share makes you a trustee; disclose more than required.
- 2023-10: "my instinct always has been in favor of the trusteeship model" (S007)
- 2018-02: Kotak reported SMA-2 loans voluntarily (S008).
- 2019-02: "the system needs to trust balance sheets if you cannot trust balance sheets there is a problem" (S009)

## Family business and the next generation
- **Stance:** Capitalism at work, socialism in the family; heirs should build operating businesses, not run family offices. Son works on merit.
- 2022-11: "a philosophy of capitalism at work but socialism within the family" (S010)
- 2025-02: "If someone has sold a business, they should be thinking about starting, buying, or building another business." (S019)
- 2025-08: "the next generation of business families is becoming too much of financial investors instead of going out there and building businesses, operating businesses." (S001)
- 2023-10: succession is for the board and regulator; son Jay on professional merit (S007).

## Manufacturing versus services and quick money
- **Stance:** Wants founders in mid-size manufacturing (Rs 50 crore to Rs 1,000 crore projects), not easy stock-market returns.
- 2025-08: "What we need is middle India in manufacturing." (S001)
- 2025-08: "Why should I build a run or operate a manufacturing company when I can make 15% in the stock market" (S001)
- 2026-05: "India has financialized too early" (S016)

## Tier-2 and Tier-3 markets
- **Stance:** Opportunity in low-cost distribution to small towns; "geography is history".
- 2014-01: would build low-cost national distribution in mobile finance, like sachets, to serve smaller tickets (S005, paraphrase).
- 2025-11: rural demand doing well, lower-end urban under pressure (S004, paraphrase).

## UPI and digital public infrastructure
- 2024-04: "I must credit India for building a world-class India stack, the UPI payment system" (S006)
- 2014-01: the large cash economy held back digital payments (S005).

## Fintech and AI lending
- **Stance:** Fintech will take profit pools; risk management stays the moat; AI-based unsecured lending unproven.
- 2020-10: "especially in fintech based and ai based lending which has happened in the indian consumer industry unsecured i think the jury is out" (S011)
- 2025-11: "not a god-given right of being a bank." (S004)

## AI
- 2025-11: like electricity; world-changing, but most first companies died: "AI is gamechanging but how to play AI is still early days." (S004)
- 2023-10: "AI is a left brain competitor it's much less a right brain competitor" (S007)

## Hiring
- **Stance:** Hire the sharp counterparty who squeezed you; hire failed founders; integrity first.
- 2014-01: "if you spotted one go after that person and that's how I built the team" (S005)
- 2017-03: "if you get a entrepreneur who has failed and wants to come back to the job market that's the best person to get" (S013)
- 2018-05: wants under-40s on the leadership team but warns of blazing years then poor risk management (S017).

## Compensation and ESOPs
- 2018-05: "the excessive variable compensation is something i'm getting more and more uncomfortable with" (S017)
- 2026-05: warns against running a company for the ESOP vesting and the stock price (S016, paraphrase).

## Culture
- 2017-03: "culture eats strategy for breakfast every single day" (S013)
- 2025-08: values must never change; products must (S001).

## Competition and consolidation
- 2023-10: "policy must encourage many flowers to bloom" (S007)
- 2019-02: "consolidation some of it will happen through mortality as well" (S009)

## Timing and crises
- **Stance:** Every crisis is an opportunity for the survivor; money is made when blood flows, but measure the blood. Newer view (2024 to 2026): the 80-year habit of reversion to the mean may break; price a small chance of structural change.
- 2014-01: "every crisis in a sector is a huge opportunity" (S005)
- 2026-03: "Money is made when blood is flowing on the street. Okay? But you got to figure out kitna khoon nikla hai." (S015)
- 2024-03: "please be ready that in your generation what if that reversion to mean does not happen how are you going to cope up with it" (S014)
- 2026-03: "in your chase for returns, keep a small pocket for fear of risk." (S015)

## Selling the company
- No recorded position on founders selling. 2025-02: those who sold should build again (S019).

## Founder exit and institutions
- 2016-11: institutions must outlive individuals (S012). He stepped down as CEO early in 2023 for a "plain white canvas" (S007).

## Focus versus diversification
- 2026-03: "if you want to make serious money, you can't be too diversified, too fragmented, too much all over the place." (S015) Yet Kotak entered every financial niche as it opened (S001). Inferred: focused on one sector, diversified within it.

## Remote work, hours, burnout
- No recorded position on burnout. 2017-03: "never have I felt in the last 30 plus years in my life that I don't feel like going to work" (S013)
