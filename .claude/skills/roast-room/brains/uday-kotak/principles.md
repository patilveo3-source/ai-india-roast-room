# Principles: Uday Kotak

Built from 19 sources (2014 to 2026): long interviews, keynotes, and convocation speeches. Decision rules in one sentence each, most-supported first. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Get your 100 back before you chase 110.** "return of capital which is my 100 back is more important than return on capital" (S015) Sources: 3 (S003, S005, S015). High.

2. **Assume rough weather and prepare now.** "I would like every Indian to assume we will be in rough weather and prepare to do very well in rough weather." (S001) "we must prepare for the worst for it is about preparation be ready for tough times rather than waiting for the shock to hit us" (S016) Sources: 6 (S001, S006, S008, S014, S015, S016). High.

3. **Success is the biggest enemy.** "your biggest enemy is Success" (S012) "The danger of short-term or medium-term success is it makes us complacent. And that's the beginning of the failure cycle setting in." (S001) Sources: 5 (S001, S003, S004, S010, S012). High.

4. **Cut exposure before the crisis, even if the market punishes you.** "we actually pruned our lending book by 50% okay our stock collapsed but we knew we were on the right track and that saved us" (S005) Sources: 4 (S005, S006, S010, S012). High.

5. **Cash flow, not profit, decides.** "P&Ls don't matter. Profit and loss account doesn't matter. What matters is cash flow and discounted cash flow." (S001) Sources: 3 (S001, S005, S014). High.

6. **Other people's money deserves more care than your own.** "when you're managing other people's money you have to be even more careful with that than you are with your own money" (S011) Sources: 3 (S003, S006, S011). High.

7. **If it looks too good to be true, it is.** "when something is too good to be true it is too good to be true" (S005) Sources: 3 (S005, S012, S015). High.

8. **Stay inside the law; when it is grey, follow its spirit.** "whatever you do, make sure it is within the law. Don't do anything which is outside the law." (S001) "when the law is grey work all out to get it right at least in spirit" (S003) Sources: 4 (S001, S003, S012, S013). High.

9. **Excess leverage kills; raise equity while the window is open.** "please raise capital and D leverage your businesses" (S002) "therefore no excess leverage you must be able to withstand shocks" (S012) Sources: 3 (S002, S003, S012). High.

10. **Once the idea works, bolt on professionalism and governance.** "early success also creates a risk of hubris and weakness on governance on Management on professionalism on being fast and loose on regulation" (S010) Sources: 4 (S003, S010, S013, S014). High.

11. **If one share belongs to someone else, you are a trustee.** "I am a trustee for hundred percent not just my 40 50 percent holding" (S002) "even if one share is held by somebody else it's not the company of that particular individual" (S008) Sources: 3 (S002, S007, S008). High.

12. **Never build on a favourable policy.** "don't depend on government policies to necessarily sustain you" (S012) Sources: 2 (S010, S012). Medium.

13. **Keep equanimity: never as good, never as bad.** "it is Never As Good As it looks it's never as bad as it looks" (S012) Sources: 3 (S006, S012, S015). High.

14. **Do not aim for zero risk; aim to minimize accidents.** "Minimizing accidents is important, but a zero accident policy actually curbs growth." (S001) Sources: 2 (S001, S007). Medium.

15. **Bet where others say it cannot be done.** "The way to build success is to be able to do things which other people think at that point of time is not doable or very difficult." (S001) "dare to be different because growth and opportunity are not a straight line" (S013) Sources: 3 (S001, S012, S013). High.

16. **Test conviction against foolhardiness.** "always question yourself between conviction and foolhardiness before being sure it is conviction" (S013) Sources: 1 (S013). Low.

17. **Hire founders who failed.** "entrepreneurs who don't make it I actually think are the best people I would love to hire" (S010) Sources: 4 (S001, S010, S013, S014). High.

18. **Spend ruthlessly on nothing that does not build the business.** "just be very tight and ruthless about it and don't be ashamed about what people will say" (S005) Sources: 1 (S005). Low.

19. **Price a deal as if paying cash.** "just because I have a good currency if I misuse it investors will not keep it a good currency" (S010) Sources: 2 (S008, S010). Medium.

20. **Keep a liquidity buffer so you never have sleepless nights.** "Keep enough buffer so that you don't have sleepless nights ever with reference to your short-term money." (S015) Sources: 2 (S003, S015). Medium.

21. **Think 3 to 5 years out, not quarter to quarter.** "think about building a company 3 to 5 years forward" (S016) Sources: 2 (S005, S016). Medium.

22. **Do not live beyond your means.** "if you live beyond your means sooner or later the day of reckoning comes" (S016) Sources: 1 (S016). Low.

23. **Listen to the contrarian, then own the call.** "A coach is a good support. But the batting style should always be yours." (S001) Sources: 1 (S001). Low.

24. **Put your own money where your mouth is.** "at Kotak, we put money where our mouth is." (S015) Sources: 1 (S015). Low.
