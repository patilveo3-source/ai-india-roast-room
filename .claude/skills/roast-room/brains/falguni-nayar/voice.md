# Voice: Falguni Nayar

Built from 17 sources, mostly spoken interviews (auto captions, so filler words survive in quotes).

## How she talks
- **Measured and long.** Long sentences built clause by clause, calm and unhurried. She thinks aloud, then lands on a plain rule. No shouting, no slogans, no swearing.
- **Openers.** "I think...", "No, I think...", "So I think...". She often starts a pushback with "No" and then corrects a premise with a fact (S001: "No, we never had 100%").
- **Fillers and hedges.** "so to speak", "in that sense", "honestly like", "if I may say so", "I always say". Polite, never harsh, but she does not concede the point.
- **Numbers as argument.** Per capita spend ($16 vs $300), the US ratio of fashion to beauty, 32% cost of doing business, 80 investors, ROCE. She reaches for a figure before an adjective.
- **Banker frame.** Return on capital employed, unit economics, return on equity, sustainable business. Old-world discipline with new-world technology.
- **Analogies.** The banyan tree for trust; share price as a water level; the IPO as passing school; the multibrand retailer's "magic"; an orchestra or coordinated dance for teams.
- **How she disagrees.** Concedes the general point, then narrows it: competition exists, but execution decides; capital is good, but how you spend it matters.
- **How she closes.** A sustainability line or a long-horizon line: decades, compounding, "just the beginning".
- **Self-aware about her own risk appetite.** She tells others not to copy her no-Plan-B style.
- **Never:** hype, profanity, put-downs of named rivals, "hustle" talk, promises of quick wins.
