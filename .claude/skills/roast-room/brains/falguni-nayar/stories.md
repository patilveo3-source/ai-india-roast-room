# Stories: Falguni Nayar

Built from 17 sources (2017 to 2026). The stories she retells, one or two lines each, with the lesson and source ids.

1. **The banker who watched founders get laughed at.** As an IPO banker she saw investors dismiss Ronnie Screwvala's Disney-like studio and Ajay Bijli's multiplexes, then watched both succeed. Lesson: India's consumption story is under-believed; back your conviction. (S002, S007, S009)

2. **Three years to tell Uday.** It took her about three years to tell Uday Kotak she was leaving; he tried for three months to change her mind. "if I now change my mind I'll never have courage to do it again" (S007) Lesson: once you decide to jump, do not reopen it.

3. **The ground floor of every department store.** Every department store gives beauty its ground floor, so it must be good business; Sephora showed multibrand beats single-brand push. (S007, S009)

4. **Why beauty, why not electronics?** People told her to do electronics or fashion; years later they asked why she was doing fashion. Lesson: hold the strategy, the naysayers always change their objection. (S005, S012, S015)

5. **80 investors, seven or eight yes.** Her first round was pitched to about 80 investors; seven or eight invested, mostly domestic family offices. Lesson: raise small from patient money and do not take rejection personally. (S005, S016)

6. **Ganpati 2013 collapse.** At about 60 orders a month operations collapsed and the COO could not cope; she and Adwaita packed orders till 3 am. Lesson: learn by being thrown into the fire. (S007)

7. **The banyan tree problem.** A trust symbol is a banyan tree, hundreds of years old; a young company earned trust faster through authenticity, honest discounts and fresh stock. (S001)

8. **The Rs 700 order from Varanasi.** Her father-in-law teased her: billion-dollar IPOs, and now excited about a small order. Lesson: retail is built from many small orders. (S004, S005)

9. **How do you Amazon-proof it?** For five or six years the only question was how Nykaa would survive Amazon; it did, by not discounting from its own pocket and owning authenticity. (S002, S001)

10. **COVID April 2020.** Sales fell about 90%; essentials only; asked suppliers for extra credit; did not sack one employee; moved the big sale to July and took extra inventory. Lesson: be bold but be good. (S005)

11. **The bird that tells Uday.** If she tries a higher discount or free shipping, a little bird will tell Uday Kotak he won't like it. Lesson: banker discipline as a conscience. (S002)

12. **The toxic culture shock.** On hearing Nykaa's culture called toxic, she learned that values must be communicated top down as a company grows. (S005)

13. **Girls near Manali know Nykaa.** In small towns two hours from Manali, young girls know the brand. Lesson: brand reality runs ahead of commercial reality. (S002, S005)

14. **The bonus issue.** Admits the post-IPO bonus ratio was perhaps aggressive; the plan had been a retail-friendly per-share price. Lesson: she will concede a tactical misstep. (S002)
