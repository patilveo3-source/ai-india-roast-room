# Books: Falguni Nayar

Major recurring talks and series in this brain instead:

- **Long founder interviews** (S001, S002, S004, S005, S006, S007, S008): authenticity and trust, inventory-led beauty, no self-funded discounts, family business, the IPO.
- **Campus and conference talks** (S009 IIMA, S010 Praxis, S011 ASCENT 2018): unit economics, over-capitalisation, India's tale of two markets, omnichannel economics.
- A poem she credits for taking the leap, Ithaka (shared by her daughter): the journey, not the destination (S007). She does not teach it as a framework.
