# Principles: N. R. Narayana Murthy

Built from 22 sources, 2004 to 2026 (interviews, fireside chats, convocation speeches, the 2004 AGM speech, a written interview, an oral history). Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **If you doubt whether to disclose, disclose.** "when in doubt disclose that is the finest coroporate governance principle that you people can follow" (S006) Sources: S006, S014, S021. High.
2. **Seek respect first; money follows.** "Respect should be the main purpose of any company because if you are respected, revenues will come." (S012) Sources: S002, S007, S011, S012, S014. High.
3. **The leader goes first and takes least.** "he or she takes much less salary uh or makes huge sacrifices in salary than other people" (S003) Sources: S003, S012, S020. High.
4. **Put the community ahead of yourself; it pays later.** "put the interest of the community out of your personal interest in everything you do in the short and medium term and you will be a winner in the long term" (S003) "At all times, you must put public good ahead of private good. This will lead to private good in the long run." (S019) Sources: S003, S012, S019. High.
5. **Governance starts at the second shareholder, not at the IPO.** "wherever there is more than one individual who is uh part of the equity pool then good corporate governance is necessary" (S002) Sources: S002, S003, S007. High.
6. **Rules do not stop crooks; values do.** "any system of regulation and compliance is as good as the people who are governed by it" (S014) "The crooks seem to be always a step ahead of the regulators." (S014) Sources: S014, S020. Medium.
7. **Culture is what you do unseen.** "culture is how I behave when I know nobody is watching me" (S014) Sources: S014, S022. Medium.
8. **A values breach ends the relationship, whatever the share price.** "this is a value system transgression therefore the best thing is to part face" (S002) "never ever be beaten by fear" (S002) Sources: S002. Low.
9. **Do not discount below what funds quality.** "if I were to reduce the prices for you, actually you will suffer because we will not be able to invest enough in attracting good employees" (S012) Sources: S003, S012, S020. High.
10. **Never lie to your team; never panic in front of them.** "the moment they think that it is all lie they won't trust you anymore" (S003) "when a child is not well at home neither of the parent shows panic" (S003) Sources: S003. Low.
11. **Hire competence, then values; trust must be total.** "nobody should say when I am sleeping the other fellow is putting his hand in the till" (S003) "if you can ensure that everybody sleeps well that unleashes tremendous energy" (S015) Sources: S003, S015. Medium.
12. **Share the upside with the people who built it.** "it cannot be that only you know four of us will make a lot of money and rest of you will suffer" (S003) Sources: S003, S021. Medium.
13. **Live austerely if you want capitalism to survive.** "They have to lead an austere life shunning vulgar display of wealth and power if they want capitalism to become strong in India." (S020) "They will eschew a life of vulgar richness." (S011) Sources: S011, S014, S020. High.
14. **Praise in public, correct in private, exit with courtesy.** "praise in public, criticize in private" (S012) Sources: S003, S012. Medium.
15. **Decisions run on data.** "He makes objective decisions based on the analysis of data." (S018) "Being an engineer, I have been a numbers man all my life." (S020) Sources: S002, S018, S020. High.
16. **Bottom line beats top line.** "I am not satisfied just with Topline growth I want even faster bottom line growth" (S007) Sources: S007, S020, S021. High.
17. **Cash in the bank is safety.** "The real happiness in a company is cash in the bank." (S021) Sources: S021. Low.
18. **Humility prevents disaster.** "Hubris is what leads people to disaster." (S014) "it is very important for you to be humble, to be courteous and to be grounded" (S012) Sources: S002, S012, S014. High.
19. **Aim high.** "A plausible impossibility is better than a convincing possibility" (S018) Sources: S018, S019. Medium.
20. **Hard work and discomfort are the price.** "Nothing worthwhile in the world can be achieved without discomfort and hard work." (S012) Sources: S001, S012, S015. High.
21. **Direction before speed.** "if you're running in the wrong direction management if you're running faster it is worse than not running at all" (S002) Sources: S002. Low.
22. **Luck is real; it should make you humble.** "luck is very very important" (S003) Sources: S003. Low.
