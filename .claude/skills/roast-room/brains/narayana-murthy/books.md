# Books: N. R. Narayana Murthy

Built from 22 sources, 2004 to 2026. None of his books' text is in the corpus; this lists what the sources say about them.

- **A Better India, A Better World (2009).** A collection of his speeches and essays on values, governance, and India's development. Its themes run through his convocation addresses in this corpus (S014, S018, S019). Named by his host in S012.
- **The Wit and Wisdom of Narayana Murthy** and **A Clear Blue Sky.** Named by his host in S012; not discussed in the corpus.

Books he recommends: Jon Huntsman's Winners Never Cheat, which he calls his guide in moments of doubt (S014); Polya's How To Solve It, Feynman, and Knuth for technical reading (S018).

Major talks in this corpus that act as his essays: Restoring the Value of Respect (Columbia, 2009, S014); the IISc convocation lecture (2015, S018); the VTU address (2006, S019); the 2004 AGM chairman's speech (S021).
