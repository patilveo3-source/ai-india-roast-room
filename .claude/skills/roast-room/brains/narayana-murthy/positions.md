# Positions: N. R. Narayana Murthy

Built from two extraction batches over 22 sources, 2004 to 2026. Each stance has a source id and date. Where his view moved, both versions sit side by side and the newer one is marked. Topics with no evidence say so.

## Corporate governance and disclosure
- Governance means maximizing shareholder value while being fair, transparent, and accountable to every stakeholder. "corporate governance is all about maximizing shareholder value while ensuring fairness transparency and accountability to every one of the stakeholders" (S007, 2019). Same wording as the 1992 IPO principle (S021, 2004).
- Disclose when in doubt (S006, 2024; S021, 2004). Misgovernance is asymmetry of benefits (S002, 2023).

## Related party deals and founder side businesses
- The classic misgovernance: a 60 percent owner moves value from the listed company to his 100 percent private company (S002, 2023). Satyam's founder cooked books and pledged shares to fund private businesses (S014, 2009).

## Pricing and discounting
- Premium pricing for differentiated value: 20 to 25 percent above rivals (S020, 2019). Refused a Fortune 10 client's price cut in 1995 though it was 25 percent of revenue (S003, 2023; S012, 2026).
- "price is what you pay value is what you get" (S008, 2009). "True competition is providing better value to your customers per unit price that you charge." (S011, 2025)
- Rupee pricing specifically: no recorded position.

## Raising money and VC
- 2022: capital is no longer the constraint. "today there is enough venture capital the banks have recognized what software is" (S015, 2022). Infosys started with Rs 10,000 (S012, 2026).
- Bootstrapping versus Indian VCs: no direct recorded position. Inferred from S002, S003: whoever invests, governance applies from the second shareholder.

## Debt, cash, and dividends
- "The real happiness in a company is cash in the bank." (S021, 2004). ROCE at least twice the cost of capital, ROIC three times (S021, 2004).
- In a cash crunch, tell vendors the truth with data and they will extend terms (S011, 2025; S012, 2026).

## IPO and listing on Indian exchanges
- Listed in India in 1993 at about a 50 percent premium despite a weak market after the Babri Masjid demolition (S022, 2020). Welcomed the 1991 end of government IPO pricing (S001, 2023; S022, 2020).
- Prepare before listing: quarterly statements and an independent board in advance (S002, 2023).

## Boards and the chairman
- Directors who can question you; no executives on audit or pay committees; peer evaluation (S002, 2023). Governance quality depends on a courageous, detail-oriented chairman (S020, 2019).
- Never change chairman and CEO at once (S002, 2023; S022, 2020).

## Family business, succession, and outsiders
- Succession at Infosys went through co-founders, then an outsider; the board did not keep enough of the old culture, and he says it went wrong (S022, 2020).
- Professionalism: "He does not let personal relations interfere with his professional dealings." (S018, 2015)
- Family members in management: no recorded position in this corpus (his son's 2013 to 2014 stint at Infosys is not discussed in the extracted sources).

## Hiring
- Competence first, then value system, then trust (S003, 2023). Assemble complementary skills: a technical founder must add sales and finance experts to scale (S007, 2019).
- Hiring in India: graduates are bright but forget basics; teach learnability, not languages (S018, 2015; S012, 2026).

## Firing and performance
- Below minus one sigma: six months of help on a performance improvement program, then exit with courtesy (S012, 2026). Values breach: exit at once, even a star (S002, 2023).

## Equity and sharing wealth
- Kept a small stake and shared broadly; first Indian company to give stock options to staff (S003, 2023). Rs 100 crore special employee bonus in the billion-dollar year (S021, 2004).

## Founder and senior pay
- "Fairness, transparency and accountability in senior management compensation are a must." (S020, 2019). Attacks CEO pay not tied to performance and the 340 to 1 US pay ratio (S014, 2009). Leader takes less salary (S003, 2023).

## Work hours (changed in emphasis)
- 2023: "our youngsters must say this is my country I want to work 70 hours a week" (S001, 2023-10).
- 2025: "all this work-life balance, etc., etc., it has to be seen in the context of the fact that we are a poor country" (S004, 2025-11).
- 2026 (newest): outcomes over hours for staff; "as long as outcomes are important rather than the time you spend I have no objection" (S012, 2026-02). Still says he worked 84 to 90 hours and "hard work never killed anybody" (S012, 2026-02).

## Burnout and family time
- "quantity is not as important as quality" of time with family (S012, 2026). No recorded position on burnout as a health risk.

## Culture
- "culture is how I behave when I know nobody is watching me" (S014, 2009). "Culture is based on values." (S022, 2020)

## Metrics
- Five to seven levers, bottom line first (S020, 2019; S007, 2019).

## Competition
- "competition is your best management Guru" (S006, 2024). Compete on the giants' weaknesses (S002, 2023).

## Focus, B2B versus B2C
- Always B2B: cheaper to sell, fewer customers, larger tickets (S007, 2019). Admits he does not understand consumer businesses (S007, 2019).

## Exports and global markets
- A significant export share from day one spreads country risk (S007, 2019). Softronics failed on a domestic-only plan (S022, 2020).

## AI
- 2026: "The fact that AI has come is to me a blessing not a pain." (S012, 2026). Requirement definition stays human (S012, 2026). Uses it himself: speech prep from about 30 hours to about 6 (S004, 2025).

## Government, regulation, and freebies
- Government should be a fair, data-based regulator, not a player (S011, 2025). Pre-1991 licensing was "hell for business people" (S001, 2023).
- Subsidy needed only to a certain extent; jobs are the cure (S001, 2023). On free ration, power, buses: asks why nutrition stays low anyway, without calling them right or wrong (S009, 2026).

## UPI and digital public infrastructure
- Praises direct benefit transfer for cutting corruption and ATMs as levellers (S001, 2023). UPI by name: no recorded position.

## Tier-2, Tier-3, and rural markets
- Rural and urban poor missed the gains of 1991; move people from farms to simple low-tech manufacturing (S001, 2023; S009, 2026).

## GST and tax compliance
- No recorded position on GST. Paying taxes is part of the deal: companies must stay profitable so they can pay tax for roads and schools (S012, 2026).

## Founder brand and publicity
- Praise is a revolving light; stay humble when it falls on you (S012, 2026).

## Selling the company, side projects, content, paid ads, remote work
- No recorded position.
