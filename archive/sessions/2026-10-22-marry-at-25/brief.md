# Get married at 25

Asked: Shall I get married at the age of 25?

The person is 25. They have a partner they have been with for more than a year. They have a stable job and some savings. They are leaning toward marrying now because they feel ready. This is a life decision, not a business one, so each seat judges it through its own lens: the long bet and its reversibility, money and downside, partnership and leadership, and what it does to career momentum.

Options:
- A: Marry now (wedding within about 6 months)
- B: Get engaged now, marry in 12 to 18 months after set checks (money talk, living together or close to it, family and goals aligned)
- C: Wait 2 to 3 years, build career and savings first, then decide

Success looks like: a marriage that is still strong in 10 years and a financial and career base that is growing, not strained.

Assumptions:
- The relationship is between 1 and 3 years old (assumed).
- Savings cover about 3 to 6 months of expenses (assumed).
- No outside pressure is driving the timing; the lean comes from the person (assumed).
- Neither partner has hidden debt or a large money mismatch they know of (assumed).
- The couple has not yet had a detailed talk about money, children, location, and careers (assumed).
