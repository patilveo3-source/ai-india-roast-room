# Round 1: Opening positions

## Kamath

SEAT: Kamath (Bootstrapping and staying profitable)
STANCE: Yes
OPTION SCORES: A 2/10 · B 8/10 · C 3/10
PILLAR SCORE: 8/10
CONFIDENCE: 4/5
RUIN RISK: none
DOOR: two-way

THE CALL: B lo, Tier-2 mein profit se grow karo, pehle 18 mahine mein family ka ₹50 lakh chukao, aur ₹8 crore abhi mat lo.

WHY:
1. Rupee unit economics (S013). Net margin 8% hai, matlab ₹100 ki bikri pe ₹8 bachte hain. Quick commerce 25 se 35% commission leta hai. Toh har ₹100 ki metro sale pe aap ₹17 se ₹27 gawa rahe ho, ads aur freebies se pehle hi. Boss, VC ka paisa yeh loss bharega, theek nahi karega. "if you spend 3000 rupees to acquire that customer and you only generated 200 rupees brokerage the business can go nowhere" (S013).
2. Money brings obligation (S013). ₹40 crore valuation matlab revenue ka 8x aur saal ke ₹38 lakh profit ka lagbhag 105x. Is number ko justify karne ke liye agla round chahiye hoga, aur uske baad ek aur. "if you get into this game you cannot stop at one round" (S014). Aur seedha poochu, aap paise ka karoge kya? Agar jawab hai "dark-store margins bharenge", toh woh plan nahi hai.
3. Runway aur zero debt (S006). C mein loan wala hissa sabse kharab hai: pehle se ₹50 lakh ka family loan hai, upar se aur debt. ₹3.2 lakh mahine ka profit hai. Revenue aadha ho gaya toh ek bhi EMI nahi chalegi. "Leverage always looks great when markets are doing well, and the risks become obvious only when things go wrong." (S001)

Ek baat maanta hoon, snacks copy karna aasaan hai, aur "if it is very easily copyable boss raise money and put some distance on your competition" (S013). Lekin aapka edge Tier-2 ka distribution aur repeat buyer hai, metro ka shelf nahi.

THE RISK I SEE: Funded brand Tier-2 mein discount lekar ghuse aur aap 8% margin pe price war nahi jhel paoge.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: Agar ek metro mein kam se kam 3 mahine tak quick commerce pe, commission ke baad bhi, contribution margin positive aaye aur repeat rate 30% se upar ho, tab chhota round banta hai, aur woh bhi industry ke angels se, VC se nahi.

WHAT I'D DO THIS WEEK: Family loan ka 18 mahine ka repayment plan likho, ₹2.8 lakh mahina, profit se.

QUESTION FOR THE FOUNDER: Kya aap khush rahoge agar business teen saal ₹5 se 8 crore pe hi atka rahe, lekin profitable aur debt-free rahe? Kyunki paisa lene ke baad obligation aati hai. Growth at what cost?

## Mittal

SEAT: Mittal (Deals, valuation, and founder grit)
STANCE: Conditional yes
OPTION SCORES: A 4/10 · B 5/10 · C 7/10
PILLAR SCORE: 7/10
CONFIDENCE: 3/5
RUIN RISK: possible
DOOR: two-way

THE CALL: C lo, lekin equity nahi, ₹2 se 3 crore revenue-based debt ya working capital line se sirf 2 metros test karo, aur VC ka ₹40 crore wala offer price discovery ki tarah warm rakho.

WHY:
1. Valuation ka math dekho boss. ₹40 crore post, ₹4.8 crore revenue pe 8x+ hai, aur 8% margin pe lagbhag ₹38 lakh saal ka profit, matlab 100x earnings. "So market determines your valuation." (S001) Ek hi VC hai, competing term sheet nahi hai. Agar metros mein plan miss hua aur next round ₹20 crore pe aaya, anti-dilution aapko kha jayega. "So that's the danger of raising money at high  valuations." (S001) Main dot-com mein sab kuch haar chuka hoon, Lehman ke baad companies bachate hue dekha hai, isliye bol raha hoon.
2. Equity ek life choice hai (Raising equity is a life choice, S001). "early stage investors  like me expect 50%." (S001) ₹8 crore ko 5 saal mein ₹60 crore banana hai, matlab company ₹300 crore+ ki. Aapka success definition 3 saal mein ₹15 crore revenue hai. Ye do alag zindagiyan hain, right? Aur quick commerce ka 25 se 35% commission 8% margin wale snack brand ko loss mein daal dega.
3. Capital ladder (S001): customer money pehle, phir debt, equity last. Aap profitable ho, Tier-2 customers already paisa de rahe hain. Metro test ka working capital debt se fund hota hai, 20% equity se nahi. Aur ek baat: VC ke paise se family ka ₹50 lakh loan chukana founder ko investors se pehle liquidity dena hai; "while your investors have not yet I think that to me is a no no" (S002). Koi serious VC ye nahi karega.

THE RISK I SEE: Debt le kar metros mein quick commerce ka CAC aur commission margin kha jaye, aur ₹38 lakh ke profit pe ₹2 se 3 crore ki repayment plus family ka loan ek saath gale pad jaye.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: Ek doosra VC ₹40 crore ya upar ka term sheet de, aur 2 metros mein 6 mahine ka data dikhaye ki quick commerce ke baad bhi contribution margin 15%+ rehta hai.

WHAT I'D DO THIS WEEK: 3 aur investors se baat karo sirf ye jaanne ke liye ki koi aur ₹40 crore dega ya nahi, aur ek revenue-based financing term sheet le aao.

QUESTION FOR THE FOUNDER: Metro ka customer aapka snack Tier-2 wale se zyada price pe kyun lega, aapka right to play kya hai wahan jahan har shelf pe 50 funded brands baithe hain?

## Kotak

SEAT: Kotak (Capital, risk, and compounding)
STANCE: Conditional yes
OPTION SCORES: A 6/10 · B 5/10 · C 4/10
PILLAR SCORE: 7/10
CONFIDENCE: 3/5
RUIN RISK: none
DOOR: one-way

THE CALL: A ka ek variant: ₹8 crore equity lo, lekin ₹50 lakh ka family loan pehle din chukao, ₹4 crore ring-fence karke bank mein rakho, aur metros plus quick commerce pe burn 9 mahine tak ₹25 lakh per month pe capped rakho.

WHY:
1. Number one, debt. Profit ₹3.2 lakh per month hai, matlab saal ka lagbhag ₹38 lakh (assumed). C ka debt route lo: ₹2.5 crore at 15% (assumed) ka interest ₹37.5 lakh saal ka banta hai, yaani poora profit chala jaata hai. Ek bura quarter aaya toh business lender ke haath mein. "therefore no excess leverage you must be able to withstand shocks" (S012). Debt nahi chahiye. Equity mein koi lender raaton-raat company band nahi kar sakta (Deleverage while the window is open, S002, S003).
2. Second, return of capital first (S015). Variant ka worst case: metros mein ₹2.25 crore doob gaye, ₹50 lakh family ko gaye, phir bhi ₹4 crore se zyada bank mein hai aur Tier-2 ka ₹38 lakh profit chal raha hai. Company bina naye paise ke bach jaati hai. Plain A mein poore ₹8 crore quick commerce mein jhonk diye toh kuch nahi bachta.
3. Third, too good to be true (S012). Quick commerce 25 se 35% commission leta hai aur aapka net margin 8% hai. Metro mein har ₹100 ki sale pe shayad ₹10 se ₹20 ka nuksaan. "there is always a sting in the tail" (S012). Isliye burn pe cap lagao. Ummeed pe mat chalo.
4. B ko credit deta hoon, sabse safe yahi hai. Lekin "a zero accident policy actually curbs growth." (S001) Saal ke ₹38 lakh profit se 3 saal mein ₹15 crore tak nahi pahunchoge.

THE RISK I SEE: Founder ka saara dhyaan metros pe chala jaata hai aur Tier-2 ka profitable engine bhookha reh jaata hai. White shirt ko itna ragdo ki dhulne se pehle phat jaaye. Term sheet mein bhi koi sting chhupa ho sakta hai.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: Agar term sheet mein 1x non-participating se zyada liquidation preference ya full ratchet nikle, ya 3 mahine baad bhi quick commerce pe per-order contribution negative rahe, toh main B pe chala jaunga.

WHAT I'D DO THIS WEEK: Top 5 SKUs ka quick commerce unit economics ek page pe likho: commission, ads, returns aur wastage ke baad har order pe kitna bachta hai.

QUESTION FOR THE FOUNDER: Agar 9 mahine baad bhi metros mein contribution positive nahi hua, toh kya aap unhe band kar doge, chahe VC push kare?

Pehle apne 100 wapas, phir 107 ki baat.

## Aadit

SEAT: Aadit (Gen-Z speed and execution)
STANCE: Conditional yes
OPTION SCORES: A 4/10 · B 3/10 · C 7/10
PILLAR SCORE: 7/10
CONFIDENCE: 3/5
RUIN RISK: possible
DOOR: two-way

THE CALL: C ka fast variant lo. Debt ya chhote round ka wait mat karo. Agle 60 din apne cash se 1 metro aur apne top Tier-2 city mein quick commerce pe live jao, phir proof ke saath VC ke paas wapas jao.

WHY:
1. Look, retention hi truth hai: "most consequential pnl metric is retention" (S004). Tier-2 mein repeat proven hai, metro quick commerce pe data zero hai. Wahan ₹8 crore jalana pre-PMF burn hai. Constrain, then attack (S004): naye channel mein pehle tight raho, proof aaye toh aggressive ho jao.
2. Rupee-in, two-out ka test (S004): "selling a dollar for 90 or are you actually investing a dollar to get $2 back in 6 to 9 months". Gross margin main assume kar raha hoon 45%, brief mein diya nahi hai. 30% commission ke baad 15% bachta hai, platform ads ke 8 se 10% ke baad sirf ~5%. Metro cohort repeat nahi kiya toh har rupee 90 paise mein bikega. Ye number ₹15 se 20 lakh mein 60 din ke andar mil jayega.
3. Tier-2 tumhara edge hai, kamzori nahi: "the Insight really for tier 2 and tier three markets is that customers are underserved" (S010). Jis city mein brand pehle se pull karta hai, wahan 10-minute shelf pe hona speed ka sabse sasta test hai.
4. B slow by habit hai. ₹15 crore in 3 saal matlab ~45% CAGR chahiye. ₹3.2 lakh/month profit se ye compounding nahi hogi.

Candidly, meri pace bahut bade capital pe chali thi, aur snacks brand pe 10x burn copy nahi karna chahiye: "it's not it's not for everybody right it is it is a high tam high stakes business" (S002). Family loan aur debt pe mera koi record nahi hai, wahan Kotak ki suno.

THE RISK I SEE: Quick commerce pe 25 se 35% commission ke saath volume aata hai, margin nahi aata. Upar se ₹50 lakh ka family loan aur burn milkar founder ka cash khatam kar dete hain.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: Metro QC cohort mein week-six repeat 25% se upar ho aur contribution fully loaded positive ho, toh A, aaj hi, poora ₹8 crore. Repeat 10% se neeche ho toh B.

WHAT I'D DO THIS WEEK: 1 metro ke 30 dark stores aur top Tier-2 city mein Blinkit, Zepto aur Instamart listing live karwao, VC se 60 din ka hold maango, aur founder khud 30 metro buyers ko call kare.

QUESTION FOR THE FOUNDER: Tier-2 mein aapka 60-day repeat rate kya hai, aur har channel ka fully loaded gross margin kitna hai?
