# VC ka paisa lu ya bootstrapped rahu?

Asked: Mera D2C snacks brand ₹40 lakh per month karta hai, profitable hai, 70% sales Tier-2 cities se. Ek VC ₹8 crore offer kar raha hai ₹40 crore valuation pe. Paisa lekar quick commerce aur metros mein jaau, ya bootstrapped rehkar Tier-2 mein hi grow karu? Family ka ₹50 lakh ka loan bhi hai.

Founder ka D2C snacks brand ₹40 lakh per month revenue karta hai (lagbhag ₹4.8 crore saal ka) aur profitable hai. 70% sales Tier-2 cities se aati hai. Ek VC ₹8 crore offer kar raha hai ₹40 crore post-money valuation pe, matlab founder 20% equity dega. Founder pe family ka ₹50 lakh ka loan hai. Sawaal hai: paisa lekar quick commerce aur metros mein expand kare, ya bootstrapped reh kar Tier-2 mein grow kare. Founder ne apna jhukaav nahi bataya.

Options:
- A: ₹8 crore lo, quick commerce aur metros mein jao
- B: Paisa mat lo, bootstrapped reh kar Tier-2 mein grow karo
- C: Chhota round ya debt lo (₹2 se 3 crore), pehle 2 metros mein test karo

Assumptions:
- Net margin around 8%, matlab lagbhag ₹3.2 lakh per month profit (assumed).
- GST registered hai, 12% ya 18% slab pe (assumed).
- Team 15 log, founder sab decisions leta hai (assumed).
- Family loan pe koi fixed repayment date nahi, lekin family wapas chahti hai (assumed).
- Quick commerce pe listing aur dark-store margins 25 se 35% commission lete hain (assumed).
- Success matlab 3 saal mein ₹15 crore annual revenue, profitable rehte hue (assumed).
