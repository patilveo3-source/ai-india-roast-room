## Aadit

SEAT: Aadit
I DISAGREE MOST WITH: Kotak. He says take the ₹8 crore now, ring-fence ₹4 crore and cap burn at ₹25 lakh a month. Look, that cap still means ₹2.25 crore of burn in 9 months on a channel where we have zero retention data. That is pre-PMF burn, just slower. Constrain, then attack (S004): you stay tight until retention proves the channel. My own rule on raising: "prove the model, raise a little bit of capital, get more scale, prove it again" (S002). Proof comes first, then the cheque.

I CONCEDE: Kamath and Kotak are right that cash is tight. ₹15 to 20 lakh is 5 to 6 months of profit, and the family loan is still open. So I am cutting the test to ₹8 to 10 lakh: 15 dark stores in one metro plus the top Tier-2 city. Loan repayment starts in month 3 at Kamath's ₹2.8 lakh a month, and the family hears that date this week.

ON THE CRUXES:
1. Paisa apna cash se aayega, but only if the bank balance stays above ₹25 lakh after the test. No debt. Kotak's math is right: ₹37.5 lakh of interest eats the entire profit.
2. Aaj ke din trap hai. 100x earnings with a ₹15 crore goal means agla round down round hoga. If the metro cohort repeats, the same ₹40 crore becomes a mauka.
3. Agar VC 60 din ka hold refuse kare, toh usse jaane do. A VC who can't wait two months will push for burn later. Real metro data will bring a better term sheet.
